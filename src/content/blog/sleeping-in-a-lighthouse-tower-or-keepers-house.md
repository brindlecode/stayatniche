Search for a lighthouse hotel and you will find two quite different things. In one, you sleep inside the tower, sometimes at the very top, under the lantern. In the other, you sleep in the keepers' house or quarters beside it, with the lighthouse next door. Both are real lighthouse stays, but a booking page rarely says which one you are getting.

This guide sorts twelve lighthouse stays in ten countries by exactly that, using each hotel's own pages and the sources recorded on our hotel pages. Some of them are not hotels at all, and we say so.

## What makes a lighthouse stay worth it

Lighthouses stand where the land ends, so the views tend to be the point: the Hudson River, the Wadden Sea, an outer-sea island or Iceberg Alley. Several stays add their own draw, such as a sauna in the tower, a hot tub on a private island, a museum with the original lens, or a tower you can climb at any time. The price of that is usually small scale and remote places, with few rooms.

## You sleep in the tower

### [Saugerties Lighthouse](/hotels/saugerties-lighthouse-united-states/): Saugerties, New York

Two second-floor double bedrooms inside the 1869 lighthouse on the Hudson River, rented together, with a shared bathroom with a composting toilet. Overnight guests can climb the operational tower at any time, and there is a small museum with the original beehive Fresnel lens. The lighthouse stands on a river shoal, reached on foot by a gentle half-mile trail, and is open Thursday to Sunday nights all year.

**Suits:** couples. **Check:** the two bedrooms are booked together, and the bathroom is shared.

### [Sitka Lighthouse](/hotels/sitka-lighthouse-united-states/): Sitka, Alaska

The whole lighthouse, built in 1983 as Rockwell Lighthouse, with two bedrooms and 1.5 baths, is rented as a private-island vacation rental. It stands on a one-acre island five minutes by boat from Sitka, with a glass-walled room, a wrap-around deck, an outdoor hot tub, a fire pit and four kayaks. The airport shuttle and water taxi both ways are included for overnight stays.

**Suits:** couples, families and friends travelling together. **Check:** this is a vacation rental of the whole building, not a hotel.

### [Vuurtoren van Harlingen](/hotels/vuurtoren-van-harlingen-netherlands/): Harlingen, Friesland

The whole restored lighthouse tower is one accommodation unit for two, over three floors, with the bed on the middle level and a 360-degree view over the town, harbour and Wadden Sea. The bathroom has a round shower and a rain shower, and breakfast with fresh rolls is served by the lighthouse keeper. It is run by Dromen aan Zee.

**Suits:** couples, romantic breaks and special occasions. **Check:** a single unit for two, so book early for the date you want.

### [Phare de Kerbel](/hotels/phare-de-kerbel-france/): Riantec, Brittany

A studio at the top of a 25-metre lighthouse, in place of the old lantern, described as the only lighthouse in France that can be lived in at its summit. The glazed top room has a 360-degree panorama, with blackout blinds on every pane. The property also has a former oil-room outbuilding and the attached keeper's house, and a private garden of over 1,000 square metres with a pool and sauna for guests' exclusive use.

**Suits:** couples, lighthouse enthusiasts and quiet getaways. **Check:** the studio is one room at the top of a tower, so expect stairs.

### [Majakkahotelli](/hotels/majakkahotelli-finland/): Kylmäpihlaja, Rauma

All 15 hotel rooms are in the Kylmäpihlaja lighthouse tower on an outer-sea island, with sea views from every room except the Economy rooms, which look onto the lighthouse terrace. The Aava Suite has windows on four sides and a balcony one floor up. There are two bookable saunas in the lighthouse, plus hot tubs and a steam barrel by the shore. The island also has eight two-person cottages, which are not in the tower.

**Suits:** couples, sauna lovers and island getaways. **Check:** book a hotel room rather than a cottage if you want the tower.

### [Belle Tout Lighthouse B&B](/hotels/belle-tout-lighthouse-united-kingdom/): Eastbourne, East Sussex

A lighthouse built in 1832 on the cliffs of Beachy Head. Only one of the six bedrooms, the Keeper's Loft, is inside the original granite tower; the other five are ordinary en-suite bedrooms in the rest of the building. The Lantern Room has a 360-degree panorama across the South Downs, and the stay is adults only, for guests aged 16 and over.

**Suits:** couples, coastal walkers and quiet getaways. **Check:** ask for the Keeper's Loft by name.

## You sleep in the keepers' house beside it

### [Corsewall Lighthouse Hotel](/hotels/corsewall-lighthouse-hotel-scotland/): Kirkcolm, Dumfries and Galloway

Eleven rooms at an automated lighthouse on Scotland's coast, with sea views and a restaurant. Six of them, named Fulmar, Galloway, Ailsa, Dunskirkloch, Mull of Kintyre and Firth of Cromarty, are in the original lighthouse keepers' quarters; two suites and the Stevenson Rooms are in separate modern buildings.

**Suits:** couples, lighthouse lovers and small groups. **Check:** only six rooms are in the keepers' quarters, and none is described as in the tower.

### [Quirpon Lighthouse Inn](/hotels/quirpon-lighthouse-inn-canada/): Quirpon Island, Newfoundland and Labrador

Ten rooms in a restored 1922 light-keeper's home at the base of a still-operating lighthouse, on the shores of Iceberg Alley. All meals and the boat transfer are included, and the island has hiking and historic remains.

**Suits:** iceberg and whale watchers and lighthouse lovers. **Check:** guests do not sleep in the tower.

### [Norah Head Lighthouse & Cottages](/hotels/norah-head-lighthouse-and-cottages-australia/): Norah Head, New South Wales

The two bookable units are the Head and Assistant Lightkeepers' Quarters, heritage-listed and beside the 1903 lighthouse, not inside it. Each has three bedrooms and room for nine guests, with verandas on both. The lighthouse is 27 metres tall, with volunteer-led tours every day between 10am and 2pm.

**Suits:** families, groups of friends and history lovers. **Check:** you stay next to the tower, not in it.

### [Fyrgaarden](/hotels/fyrgaarden-denmark/): Anholt, Central Denmark Region

The former home of the lighthouse keeper and his family, next to the old lighthouse on eastern Anholt, let as three separate apartments or as the whole house. There is no electricity: oil lamps for light, gas fridges and stoves, and water from a hand pump. An artist residency is offered alongside the accommodation.

**Suits:** groups, families, artists and quiet getaways. **Check:** guests do not sleep in the tower, and the stay is off-grid.

### [STF Berguddens Fyrvaktarbostäder](/hotels/stf-berguddens-fyrvaktarbostader-sweden/): Holmön, Västerbotten

Five rooms with 11 to 25 beds in the former lighthouse keepers' dwellings beside Bergudden lighthouse, whose light has guided ships for over 130 years, at the end of the road on Holmön. It is reached by ferry from Norrfjärden, or by hovercraft over thick winter ice. There is no internet or running water, an outdoor toilet and self-catering kitchens.

**Suits:** quiet nature stays, self-catering groups and history lovers. **Check:** this is simple, and you sleep beside the lighthouse, not in it.

### [Lindesnes Lighthouse](/hotels/lindesnes-lighthouse-norway/): Lindesnes, Agder

Although the stay is listed as a hotel, there is no hotel here. The only accommodation is one holiday apartment that sleeps six, in half of the former assistant keeper's house at Norway's oldest lighthouse, first lit in 1656, on the southernmost point of the Norwegian mainland. The other half is still the home of the duty keeper, and keepers are on duty around the clock. There is a lighthouse museum in a visitor centre built into the rock.

**Suits:** lighthouse enthusiasts and maritime history lovers. **Check:** availability is very limited with a single apartment, and the site is remote, about 10 kilometres from the village of Høllen.

## What to check before you book

- **Tower or keepers' house.** Only the first six stays above put you in the tower, and at Belle Tout it is a single room.
- **Hotel or rental.** Sitka, Harlingen, Saugerties, Lindesnes and Fyrgaarden are single units or whole-property lets rather than hotel rooms.
- **Facilities.** Saugerties has a shared bathroom, Fyrgaarden has no electricity, and Bergudden has no running water or internet.
- **Getting there.** Saugerties is a half-mile walk, Sitka and Quirpon need a boat, and Holmön needs a ferry or, over winter ice, a hovercraft.
- **Dates.** Saugerties takes guests Thursday to Sunday nights; for the rest, confirm opening dates with the property.
