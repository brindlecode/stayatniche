Many Cappadocia hotels have "cave" in the name, and the word does not always mean the same thing. At some properties every room is carved into the rock. At others the cave rooms are a handful among stone-built ones, and at least one calls itself a cave hotel while its official room pages describe arched cut stone. Booking the right one starts with that distinction.

This guide covers twelve stays, grouped by how much of the hotel is cave. For each we use what the hotel says about its own rooms, as recorded on its page here, and we say where a hotel does not spell it out. Nothing below is a guarantee of a particular room, so confirm the room type when you book.

## Cave room or stone room: why it matters here

Most of the hotels below are in and around Göreme, Uçhisar and Ürgüp, and several mention fairy chimneys, the rock formations the area is known for, in their own descriptions. Many properties combine cave rooms cut into the rock with rooms built of stone, sometimes in the same restored house. So the label on a booking page is worth checking against the hotel's own room descriptions.

The terraces matter almost as much as the rooms. Several of these hotels highlight terraces for watching the morning balloons.

## Hotels where the cave is the whole stay

### [Gamirasu Cave Hotel](/hotels/gamirasu-cave-turkey/): Ayvalı

Gamirasu occupies a restored Byzantine monastic retreat in the village of Ayvalı, with 35 individually designed cave rooms and suites and a historic church within the grounds. It sits in a quieter valley with far fewer visitors than Göreme or Uçhisar, about 20 km from the Göreme Open Air Museum. Rates run roughly $180 to $380 a night.

**Suits:** couples who want a calm, historic base, and anyone happy to trade a village walk for atmosphere. **Check:** Ayvalı is more remote, so most excursions need a car or taxi, and some rooms are atmospheric but compact. Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to November) are the best months; summer is hot but the cave rooms stay cool, and in winter balloon flights can depend on the weather.

### [Melek Cave Hotel](/hotels/melek-cave-hotel-turkey/): Göreme

The official site states that every room is carved from rock and lists six cave room types, from Deluxe Cave to Cave Family. It stands in the historic centre of Göreme among the fairy chimneys, a short walk from the Göreme Open Air Museum, with free breakfast on the terrace and a free transfer from the bus station.

**Suits:** independent travellers, budget-aware couples and walkers. **Check:** the "every room" claim comes from the hotel's own site, so ask for a cave room type by name when you book.

### [Utopia Cave Cappadocia](/hotels/utopia-cave-cappadocia-turkey/): Ürgüp

Built on carved rock cavities, Utopia describes its rooms as restored cave rooms that keep Cappadocia's architecture, each with distinctive original details. It is a member of the LMHW Collection and has a spa and the Yamas restaurant on site.

**Suits:** couples, design lovers and honeymooners. **Check:** the hotel does not state its room count on the pages we read, so confirm the room category rather than assuming a size.

### [Romantic Cave Hotel](/hotels/romantic-cave-hotel-turkey/): Ürgüp

A small address: seven rooms, all described by the hotel as historic cave rooms. The hotel says it opened in 1970 with cave rooms and later renovated them. Rooms listed run from 30 to 50 square metres, and there are terraces and jacuzzi suites.

**Suits:** couples and honeymooners who prefer seven rooms to thirty-five. **Check:** the cave description is the hotel's own, so confirm the room category when you book. Free Wi-Fi, breakfast and 24-hour reception are listed.

### [Sultan Cave Suites](/hotels/sultan-cave-suites-turkey/): Göreme

Sultan has 35 rooms on Aydınlı Hill above Göreme, built over old cave houses with the original cave walls preserved. The hotel calls its lodgings "cave and stone" and gives no per-room split. Many rooms and terraces have balloon views, and the hotel says its Seten restaurant is listed in the 2026 Michelin Guide. Family suites are available.

**Suits:** couples, food lovers and families. **Check:** ask which rooms are cave and which stone, since the hotel does not say.

### [Seki Cave Hotel](/hotels/seki-cave-hotel-turkey/): Çavuşin

Opened in 2015 by the Kelebek Group, Seki is a cave boutique hotel in Çavuşin, about 3 km from Göreme. "Seki" means terraced platforms in Turkish, and the private terraces for sunrise, sunset and balloon views are the point. There is a rooftop jacuzzi, and the hotel is pet-friendly.

**Suits:** couples seeking quiet and pet owners. **Check:** the hotel presents cave suites and deluxe cave rooms, but some twin and double rooms are not individually labelled cave.

### [Cappadocia Inans Cave Suites](/hotels/cappadocia-inans-cave-suites-turkey/): Nar

Ten rooms, each described as a carefully decorated cave suite, plus a heated pool the hotel calls a cave pool. Breakfast is an open buffet of local dishes, and a private or shared transfer from Nevşehir Airport is offered.

**Suits:** couples, small-hotel fans and anyone planning a balloon flight. **Check:** the hotel does not describe how its rooms were carved, only that each is a cave suite.

### [Signature Cave Cappadocia](/hotels/signature-cave-cappadocia-turkey/): Mustafapaşa

A boutique property in the Wyndham Trademark Collection whose Premium, Junior and Deluxe suites are marketed as cave suites with natural rock walls. The Signature Pool Suite has direct access to the pool area and a private outdoor sitting area.

**Suits:** couples who want comfort and design alongside the cave. **Check:** the Signature Pool Suite is not itself labelled a cave suite, so choose the room type deliberately.

## Hotels where only some rooms are cave

### [Museum Hotel](/hotels/museum-hotel-cappadocia/): Uçhisar

Thirty-four cave and stone rooms and suites furnished with antiques, with panoramic valley views. Deluxe Rooms are restored ancient caves, while some units are stone: the Konak Pool Suite is a detached stone house. The hotel says its Lil'a Restaurant is selected by the Michelin Guide, and its outdoor pool is kept at 30 to 32 °C in winter. Rates run roughly $400 to $900 a night.

**Suits:** couples, history enthusiasts and food lovers. **Check:** book a Deluxe Room or a named cave suite if the cave is what you came for.

### [Kelebek Special Cave Hotel](/hotels/kelebek-special-cave-hotel-turkey/): Göreme

Thirty-six rooms mixing cave, fairy-chimney and stone rooms. The hotel says some cave rooms are carved directly into natural rock, including historic spaces dating to the 8th century, and that others are stone architecture. It has a traditional Turkish bath and an outdoor pool, accepts pets, and its concierge arranges activities and balloon rides.

**Suits:** couples, pet owners and balloon watchers. **Check:** ask for a carved cave room by name, since the rest are stone.

### [Ottoman Cave Suites](/hotels/ottoman-cave-suites-turkey/): Göreme

Thirteen rooms: eight rock-carved and five standard stone rooms. Five of the rock-carved suites face Uçhisar Castle. Breakfast is served on a terrace with fairy-chimney views, and the centre of Göreme is easy to reach.

**Suits:** couples, walkers and first-time visitors. **Check:** the five stone rooms are plain standard rooms, so say you want a rock-carved one.

### [Vintage Cave House](/hotels/vintage-cave-house-turkey/): Göreme

An old Göreme house owned by one family for five generations, turned into a boutique hotel in 2015. It has four restored cave rooms, which the hotel says are carved into ancient rocks, and five stone rooms, with open and closed terraces for the balloons and a peaceful garden. Nevşehir airport is about 30 minutes away.

**Suits:** couples and families who value local history. **Check:** only four of nine rooms are cave rooms.

## What to check before you book

- **The room type, not the hotel name.** Where a hotel has mixed rooms, only a named cave room type gets you the cave.
- **Cave in name only.** [Lucky Cave Cappadocia](/hotels/lucky-cave-cappadocia-turkey/) says on its official room pages that its rooms are built of arched cut stone, and none is described as carved or in a natural cave. [Georgios Cave Cappadocia](/hotels/georgios-cave-cappadocia-turkey/) is a registered Greek mansion in stone, where only Room 105 is named a cave suite. They may suit other travellers, but check the room descriptions if the cave is your reason for coming.
- **Hotels with very few cave rooms.** [Urgup Inn Cave Hotel](/hotels/urgup-inn-cave-hotel-turkey/) names only room 105 a cave room, and [Göreme Valley Cave House](/hotels/goreme-valley-cave-house-turkey/) has three underground rooms carved into real rock among many others.
- **Getting around.** At Gamirasu in Ayvalı, most excursions need a car or taxi; at Melek you can walk to the Göreme Open Air Museum.
- **Balloon flights.** Hotels often help arrange them, but flights can depend on the weather, particularly in winter.
