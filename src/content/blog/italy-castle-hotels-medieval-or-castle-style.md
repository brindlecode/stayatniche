In Italy the word "castello" on a hotel can mean very different things. It might be a hilltop fortress whose rooms sit inside the original walls. It might be a castle rebuilt over centuries, where only part of the hotel is in the old building. Or it might be a villa that was built to look like a castle in the 19th or 20th century. All three can be lovely places to stay, but only the first is what most people picture when they book a castle.

This guide sorts fifteen Italian castle stays by what each hotel says about its own building, using the hotels' own pages and the sources recorded on our hotel pages. Where a hotel does not say which rooms are in the castle, we say so.

## Why Italy suits a castle stay

The fifteen places here spread from the Alps to Sicily: Piedmont and the Langhe hills, South Tyrol, Tuscany, Umbria, Abruzzo, Apulia, Liguria and the far south of Sicily. Piedmont alone has four of them, which makes a castle-to-castle trip through the wine country possible. Several hotels pair the building with a pool, a spa or a restaurant, so a castle night can also be a base for a few days.

## Medieval walls: rooms inside the old castle

### [Castello di Montaldo](/hotels/castello-di-montaldo-italy/): Montaldo Torinese, Piedmont

All 39 rooms and suites, 20 doubles and 19 suites, are in a medieval castle founded around 1011 to 1038 and later turned into a villa and a Jesuit college. Some suites are in its towers and in the former chapel. A 3,000 m² spa offers sauna rituals led by sauna masters, and Turin is about 20 minutes away.

**Suits:** couples, spa breaks and weddings. **Check:** the hotel is a 4-star Superior, so expect a full-service hotel rather than a small castle.

### [Castello di Septe](/hotels/castello-di-septe-italy/): Mozzagrogna, Abruzzo

A restored early-medieval hilltop castle from the Lombard period, 6th to 8th century, on a rocky spur above the Sangro valley and declared a national monument. The hotel offers 45 rooms inside the castle, including six junior suites and six suites, some with whirlpool baths. There is a swimming pool and a restaurant of Abruzzo cooking.

**Suits:** couples, history lovers and weddings. **Check:** at 45 rooms it is a large castle hotel, not an intimate one.

### [Castello di Gargonza](/hotels/castello-di-gargonza-italy/): Monte San Savino, Tuscany

Less a single castle than a fortified medieval village, with a 13th-century tower and a first record from 1150. Guests sleep in restored peasant houses inside the walls, as rooms, suites and apartments, and in the medieval tower. The wooded estate of about 500 hectares has GPS-tracked walking trails, and the village sits between Arezzo and Siena with views over the Val di Chiana.

**Suits:** couples, walkers and wine lovers. **Check:** your room is a village house, so ask which building and which floor.

### [Castello di Monterone](/hotels/castello-di-monterone-italy/): Perugia, Umbria

Eighteen rooms in an old medieval castle converted into a historic residence (residenza d'epoca); the hotel says it is a real castle. Rooms are named for castle features, such as the drawbridge, arrow slits and keys, and one romantic room keeps original frescoes and panoramic windows. The historic centre of Perugia is three kilometres away.

**Suits:** couples and history lovers. **Check:** every room has its own story, so look at the room descriptions before choosing.

### [Castello di Casamassella](/hotels/castello-di-casamassella-italy/): near Otranto, Apulia

An authentic medieval castle, a fortified residence from the time of Charles of Anjou. Five suites and one double room are on different floors of the castle itself, each with private outdoor space, and the castle can also be booked for exclusive-use self-catering. The suites have no TV or telephone, for quiet, and there is a pool area reserved for guests.

**Suits:** couples seeking a quiet getaway. **Check:** six rooms in total, so availability is limited, and the no-TV approach is deliberate.

### [Schloss Hotel Wehrburg](/hotels/schloss-hotel-wehrburg-italy/): Prissian, South Tyrol

All 20 rooms, three singles and 17 doubles, are inside the medieval castle, in two 18-metre towers and the palas, and run from 15 to 47 m². By design there is no air conditioning, TV or minibar in the rooms. An infinity pool looks across the Adige valley.

**Suits:** couples, quiet retreats and history lovers. **Check:** the hotel is open seasonally, from April to late October, so plan around that.

### [Castello di Proceno](/hotels/castello-di-proceno-italy/): Proceno, Lazio

An albergo diffuso (a hotel spread over several buildings) in a castle complex: a pentagonal fortress with a keep, two turrets and a working drawbridge. Apartments and suites are in the fortino and the courtyard, with further rooms in a former tower of the old city walls. The Cecchini family has lived here since 1644. The pool and summer restaurant are reached by a passage cut in volcanic rock, and the Fonteverde thermal baths are close.

**Suits:** couples, culture lovers and walkers on the Via Francigena. **Check:** only some rooms are in the castle; the separate farmhouse Il Giulione is outside it.

### [Hotel Castello di Santa Vittoria](/hotels/hotel-castello-di-santa-vittoria-italy/): Santa Vittoria d'Alba, Piedmont

A 4-star hotel with 38 rooms inside the walls of a castle complex with over 800 years of history, with a tower looking over the Langhe and Roero hills and a restaurant, Il Castello, inside the walls. The rooms are accessible without steps and have walk-in showers.

**Suits:** couples, wine lovers and weddings. **Check:** the source does not split the 38 rooms by building within the complex.

## Castles with later layers

### [Castello di Guarene](/hotels/castello-di-guarene-italy/): Guarene, Piedmont

A Baroque castle begun in 1726 on the site of a medieval fort, with twelve restored historic rooms, formal Italian gardens designed in 1740 and a spa. Rooms are all Castle rooms or suites plus a Classic room, some beside the oldest spaces. The views run over the UNESCO-listed hills.

**Suits:** couples, honeymooners and special occasions. **Check:** this is an 18th-century castle, not a medieval one.

### [Real Castello](/hotels/real-castello-italy/): Verduno, Piedmont

A hotel in the Verduno castle since 1953. Six rooms, two suites, three junior suites and a double, are on the upper floors of the castle wing, reached only by the old staircases; the wing was renovated in the 1730s to a design linked to Juvarra. Other rooms are in the adjacent Castalderia and Nando Foresteria annexes. Views take in the Langhe and the Tanaro valley.

**Suits:** couples, food and wine lovers and history lovers. **Check:** book a castle-wing room by name, and note the staircases.

### [Castello la Leccia](/hotels/castello-la-leccia-italy/): Castellina in Chianti, Tuscany

A castle recorded in written sources since 1077, made villa-like in the 18th century and restored in 2012. There are 12 rooms of three types, from 21 to 55 m², on an organic estate that produces its own wine and olive oil, with a panoramic pool open from May to September, a spa and a restaurant. The adjacent Casa La Leccia apartment is a separate building.

**Suits:** couples, honeymooners and wine lovers. **Check:** the hotel's site never states exactly which building holds the 12 rooms.

### [Schloss Hotel Korb](/hotels/schloss-hotel-korb-italy/): Missian, Eppan, South Tyrol

A castle with a modern residence alongside it, with views of Bolzano and the Dolomites. Castle suites, about 40 m² and mostly in the historic Wohnturm, and some comfort doubles are in the castle; panorama suites, exclusive doubles and South Tyrolean and garden suites are in the newer residence. Castle terrace suites are reached only by stairs. Indoor and outdoor pools are open daily.

**Suits:** couples, spa breaks and hikers. **Check:** choose a castle suite by name, since the residence rooms are modern.

## Castle-style, not medieval

### [Castello Tafuri Charming Suites](/hotels/castello-tafuri-italy/): Portopalo di Capo Passero, Sicily

This is not a medieval castle. It is a villa built in the form of a castle, designed in 1933 and begun in 1935, and a hotel since 1964. It has eighteen rooms and suites, some with balconies set between the battlements and views of a tonnara and the sea, and a panoramic pool with a sun terrace.

**Suits:** couples, families and sea lovers. **Check:** expect a 20th-century seaside villa, not a fortress.

### [Hotel Castello Miramare](/hotels/hotel-castello-miramare-italy/): Pegli, Genoa

Not a medieval castle either: the hotel's own site says it stands on the foundations of a medieval fortlet and was later turned into a neo-Gothic villa with a tower, dated to around 1880. This 3-star hotel is directly on the beach at Pegli, most rooms face the sea, and the Navebus ferry from Genoa's Porto Antico takes under 30 minutes.

**Suits:** couples, beach stays and city breaks. **Check:** the room count is not stated.

### [Locanda al Castello](/hotels/locanda-al-castello-italy/): Cividale del Friuli

The "castle" is not a medieval fortress: it is a crenellated castle-style villa from the mid-19th century, built as the Jesuit Villa Betlemme. The 27 rooms are split between the old manor and a modern wellness wing, with a wellness centre that has an indoor pool, a Kneipp path and a hot tub. The hotel is accessible for guests with reduced mobility.

**Suits:** couples, wellness breaks and weddings. **Check:** the split between manor and wellness wing is not given, so ask for a manor room.

## What to check before you book

- **Which building your room is in.** At Proceno, Real Castello, Korb and La Leccia, part of the hotel is outside the castle itself.
- **Medieval or castle-style.** Tafuri, Miramare and Locanda al Castello are not medieval castles; Guarene is Baroque.
- **Seasons.** Wehrburg opens from April to late October, and La Leccia's pool from May to September.
- **Stairs and access.** Real Castello's castle rooms and Korb's castle terrace suites are reached only by stairs; Santa Vittoria's rooms are step-free.
- **Size.** Septe and Montaldo are large castle hotels; Casamassella has six rooms.
