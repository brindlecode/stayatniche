Most train hotels are journeys: you board in the evening and wake somewhere else. The stays in this guide go the other way. The carriage stays where it is, at a station or on a siding, and you sleep in it for as long as you like. Some are original sleeping cars with their compartments intact. Others are boxcars, cabooses or work wagons rebuilt as small apartments or studios.

We have sorted seven of them by what you actually sleep in, using each hotel's own pages and the sources recorded on our hotel pages. For the moving luxury trains, see our guide to [train hotels and luxury rail journeys](/blog/train-hotels-rail-journeys-guide/).

## Why a parked carriage works

A parked carriage gives you the railway without the timetable. Several of these places sit beside a working or heritage line, so the trains are part of the view: the Jonsdorf and Oybin carriages stand at stations on the Zittau narrow-gauge steam railway, and the carriages at Petworth have a platform with afternoon tea. A few are in hiking or cycling country, including the Ore Mountains and the Thaya cycling route.

The catch is that these are small places, and several are seasonal.

## Sleeping-car compartments and Pullman carriages

### [Wolkensteiner Zughotel](/hotels/wolkensteiner-zughotel-germany/): Wolkenstein, Saxony

Guests sleep in original sleeping-car compartments, ten in first and second class, plus a government compartment and two named suites, and in five converted wagons fitted as holiday apartments with a kitchen and bathroom. A restaurant is in a salon car, and a meeting room is in a preserved salon car from the former East German government train. Two further apartments are in the station building of 1865, and there are 54 sleeping places in total.

**Suits:** railway enthusiasts, cyclists and motorcyclists, and couples. **Check:** the station-building apartments are ordinary rooms, so choose a compartment or a wagon if the train is the point.

### [The Old Railway Station](/hotels/the-old-railway-station-united-kingdom/): Petworth, West Sussex

Eight of the ten en-suite rooms are in restored Pullman railway carriages dating from 1912 to 1923, named Mimosa, Alicante, Flora and Montana: two King Pullman and six Standard Pullman rooms. The other two rooms, Station House Lower and Upper, are in the old station building, along with a self-catering Station Masters Suite. Evening dining is in the 1906 Pullman carriage Princess Ena, and afternoon tea is served in the Waiting Room or on the platform. It lies in the South Downs, within reach of Goodwood, Chichester and Midhurst.

**Suits:** couples, railway enthusiasts and racing and polo fans. **Check:** two rooms are not in carriages, so book a Pullman room by name.

## Boxcars and cabooses

### [Northern Rail Traincar Inn](/hotels/northern-rail-traincar-inn-united-states/): Two Harbors, Minnesota

All 15 guest rooms are in real renovated train boxcars, Porter rooms and Conductor suites, each with a private bathroom and linked by an enclosed hallway that recalls an old station platform. The rooms are themed: Victorian, Oriental, Safari, Fishing, Lighthouse, Bear and Moose. A depot-style main building with a fireplace serves breakfast. It is about 35 minutes from Duluth, near Gooseberry Falls and Split Rock Lighthouse.

**Suits:** families. **Check:** choose a themed room that suits you, as they differ.

### [Train Station Inn](/hotels/train-station-inn-canada/): Tatamagouche, Nova Scotia

Suites in vintage Canadian-built cabooses and boxcars, redesigned inside with nods to their railway past, beside an original station building from around 1887 that houses a cafe and gift shop. Caboose #5 dates from 1912 and has a cupola lookout with a reading nook. The Trans Canada Trail runs directly from the station along Tatamagouche Bay, and there is live music on the station platform two or three nights a week in summer.

**Suits:** rail enthusiasts, couples and families. **Check:** the inn is seasonal; its 2026 booking season ran from 1 May to 11 October, so confirm dates for the year you plan to go.

## Carriages as apartments and studios

### [Ferienwaggons Zittauer Schmalspurbahn](/hotels/ferienwaggons-zittauer-schmalspurbahn-germany/): Jonsdorf and Oybin, Saxony

Two passenger carriages converted into holiday apartments for two, each with a kitchen, shower, bedroom and terrace, on the narrow-gauge steam railway. The hotel lists Jonsdorf station for Easter to July and Oybin station for August to October, and the terrace is in a prime spot to watch the trains.

**Suits:** rail enthusiasts and families. **Check:** which station you stay at depends on the month, and these are self-catering apartments for two.

### [Vagony na horách](/hotels/vagony-na-horach-czechia/): Kovářská, Ústí nad Labem Region

Four retired railway wagons, Alois, Fanynka, Lesní and Přednostův, fitted as insulated studios on the unused fourth track at Kovářská station. The station is 850 metres above sea level on the Ore Mountains ridge. Each wagon has a kitchen, a small bathroom with hot shower and large panoramic windows, and the wagons are used for stays even in the hardest winter. Rental income helps pay for restoring the 19th-century station building.

**Suits:** train lovers, couples, hikers and cross-country skiers. **Check:** a fifth stay here, Vechtrovna, is a former trackman's house, not a wagon.

### [ZUGHOTEL Waldkirchen](/hotels/zughotel-waldkirchen-austria/): Waldkirchen an der Thaya, Lower Austria

Two former ÖBB construction-train wagons at the station, converted to sleeping wagons with room for eight guests in total: four single rooms in one, and one double and two single rooms in the other. Breakfast is delivered to the wagon, with milk and baked goods from regional producers. There is free parking and a bicycle room with e-bike charging at the station, in the region of the 111-kilometre Thayarunde cycling route.

**Suits:** cyclists. **Check:** the stay is seasonal, and the 2026 season ended on 21 September.

## What to check before you book

- **Hotel or apartment.** The Zittau carriages and the Czech wagons are self-catering with a kitchen; Wolkenstein and Petworth are closer to hotels.
- **Which rooms are in the train.** Wolkenstein and Petworth also have rooms in the station building, and Vagony na horách has a fifth stay that is not a wagon.
- **Seasons.** Several are seasonal: the Zittau carriages are listed at two stations in different months, Train Station Inn and ZUGHOTEL Waldkirchen run seasons, and Vagony na horách's wagons are insulated for winter.
- **Size.** Waldkirchen sleeps eight in total, so book early in the cycling season.
- **Booking.** Only The Old Railway Station has an exact Expedia page; for the others the rates button may lead to the hotel's own site or a search.
