"Castle hotel" covers a lot of ground in Scotland. A few stays put you inside walls that are centuries old. Many more are Victorian or Georgian houses built to look like castles, and in several of these only some of the rooms are in the main building at all. Neither is a bad way to spend a night, but they are different stays, and the booking page rarely tells you which one you are getting.

This guide sorts twelve Scottish castle stays by what each hotel says about its own building. We have used the hotels' own pages and the sources recorded on our hotel pages, and where a hotel does not say which rooms are in the castle, we say so.

## Why Scotland suits a castle stay

The twelve properties here run from Argyll and the Highlands to the Scottish Borders and the outskirts of Edinburgh, so a castle stay can be a Highland road trip or a night close to the capital. Dalhousie Castle and Melville Castle are both near Edinburgh, and Dornoch Castle Hotel sits on the North Coast 500 route. Several hotels list dog owners among their guests, and a few can be booked whole for a group, which suits families and celebrations as much as couples.

## Old walls: sleeping inside the original castle

### [Barcaldine Castle](/hotels/barcaldine-castle-united-kingdom/): near Oban, Argyll

An early 17th-century L-plan tower house, the "Black Castle", completed by 1609, with walls about nine feet thick, two secret stairs and a dungeon. All six guest rooms are inside the castle, and it can be taken as bed and breakfast or as the whole castle for exclusive use. It stands on the south shore of Loch Creran, looking towards the Glencoe mountains.

**Suits:** couples, history enthusiasts and small private gatherings. **Check:** six rooms means limited choice, and the whole-castle option changes how the booking works.

### [Ethie Castle](/hotels/ethie-castle-united-kingdom/): near Arbroath, Angus

A 14th-century castle, with records from the early 13th century and a monks' sandstone tower house, offered for guest stays and exclusive use. The official site lists fourteen named suites plus an attached two-bedroom apartment, with suites named after admirals, ships and historical figures. There is a small chapel and a sitting room with a secret staircase.

**Suits:** groups and celebrations, couples and history lovers. **Check:** there is a three-night minimum stay.

### [Dalhousie Castle](/hotels/dalhousie-castle-united-kingdom/): Bonnyrigg, near Edinburgh

A castle that has stood for over 800 years and is one of Scotland's oldest inhabited castles, with 35 refurbished en-suite bedrooms and a Dungeon Restaurant. The Castle Classic, Deluxe, Junior Suite, Four Poster, Suite and two Feature Rooms are in the castle itself, including a Feature Room with a vaulted well. The Lodge rooms are a separate modern building in the grounds.

**Suits:** couples, special occasions and Edinburgh visitors. **Check:** ask for a Castle room, not a Lodge room, if the old walls are the point. The hotel does not say how many rooms are in each building.

### [Dornoch Castle Hotel](/hotels/dornoch-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Dornoch, Sutherland

Nine of the 21 rooms are inside the 15th-century castle; the other 12 are in a modern extension. Castle rooms have views of the garden and Dornoch Cathedral, which stands directly opposite. The hotel has a whisky bar with a wide selection of rare whiskies and a garden restaurant over the walled gardens.

**Suits:** whisky lovers, couples and North Coast 500 travellers. **Check:** fewer than half the rooms are in the castle, so request one explicitly.

### [Tulloch Castle Hotel](/hotels/tulloch-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Dingwall, Highland

The hotel dates the castle to the 12th century and offers 22 en-suite bedrooms, each with its own character, a 250-year-old panelled Great Hall and restored original fireplaces and ceilings. Dogs are welcome in all bedrooms and in the Green Lady Bar.

**Suits:** couples, dog owners and weddings. **Check:** the hotel does not say which wing each bedroom is in, so ask before you choose a room type.

## Historic house or castle-style mansion

### [Barony Castle Hotel](/hotels/barony-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Eddleston, Scottish Borders

A historic Scots-baronial house of 1536 on the site of a peel tower, with a Jacobean facade of about 1700 to 1715, a 19th-century wing and a fire in 1983, and the rest is 20th-century additions. There are 82 bedrooms including six individual suites, a 10-metre pool with a whirlpool bath and sauna, and 25 acres of grounds.

**Suits:** families, couples and weekend breaks. **Check:** much of the hotel is modern, and the source does not say which of the 82 rooms are in the old house.

### [Inverlochy Castle Hotel](/hotels/inverlochy-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Fort William

This is not a medieval castle. It is a baronial mansion built in 1863 by William Scarlett, 3rd Baron Abinger, about two miles from the 13th-century Inverlochy Castle that gave it its name. Castle rooms and suites are in the 1863 building, with views of the grounds and loch. The Walled Garden rooms are in a converted stable block five minutes away, each with a balcony or terrace, and the Gate Lodge is a separate Victorian lodge. The Seasgair dining experience is by Michel Roux.

**Suits:** couples, special occasions and food lovers. **Check:** choose a castle room or suite if you want to sleep in the main building.

### [Stonefield Castle](/hotels/stonefield-castle-united-kingdom/): Tarbert, Argyll

A Scottish baronial castle-style mansion of 1837, designed by William Henry Playfair, with wood panelling, ornate ceilings and marble fireplaces from the original house. It has 38 en-suite bedrooms in 60 acres of woodland gardens on Loch Fyne, with a rhododendron and magnolia collection planted from the 1850s.

**Suits:** couples, garden lovers and dog owners. **Check:** the hotel does not itemise which wing each bedroom is in.

### [Melville Castle](/hotels/melville-castle-united-kingdom/): Lasswade, near Edinburgh

A three-storey Gothic castle-style mansion of 1786 to 1791, in a private 54-acre estate; the medieval tower castle that stood on the site was demolished in 1780. Guests stay in the 33 bedrooms and suites of the castle itself, many with four-poster beds. Afternoon tea is served daily from 2pm to 5pm.

**Suits:** couples, families, dog owners and Edinburgh visitors. **Check:** it is a Georgian mansion, not a medieval fortress.

### [Fonab Castle Hotel](/hotels/fonab-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Pitlochry, Perthshire

A castle built in 1892 in Highland Perthshire. The Castle Suite, at the summit of the castle tower, and the Castle Room Superior are in the original castle; the other rooms are in contemporary Lochside and Woodland lodge buildings. There are 42 bedrooms across four locations, a Woodland Spa and a grill with loch views.

**Suits:** couples, spa breaks and special occasions. **Check:** most rooms are in the lodges, so book the castle suite or room by name.

## Whole castle and self-catering

### [Aldourie Castle](/hotels/aldourie-castle-united-kingdom/): Loch Ness

A baronial castle on the shores of Loch Ness that is booked for exclusive use only: the whole castle sleeps 24 guests in 12 en-suite bedrooms and is fully staffed, with meals from in-house chefs using produce from the castle gardens. There is a sauna yurt by the loch.

**Suits:** groups, families and celebrations. **Check:** you cannot book a single room.

### [Torrisdale Castle Estate](/hotels/torrisdale-castle-estate-united-kingdom/): Kintyre, Argyll

A Gothic castle begun in 1815 for General Keith Macalister and designed by James Gillespie Graham. Only The Servants' Quarters, a three-bedroom self-catering apartment that sleeps six, with a hot tub and sauna, is in the castle. The two cottages and two eco-bothies on the estate are elsewhere. The estate also has a working distillery making Kintyre Gin.

**Suits:** families, groups, pet owners and wildlife watchers. **Check:** this is self-catering, and only one unit is in the castle.

## What to check before you book

- **Which building your room is in.** Where a castle has a lodge or extension, the room type decides whether you sleep in the old walls.
- **Exclusive-use options.** Barcaldine, Ethie and Aldourie can be taken whole, which changes how the booking works.
- **Minimum stays.** Ethie asks for three nights.
- **Dogs.** Tulloch welcomes dogs in all bedrooms; Stonefield, Melville and Torrisdale list dog or pet owners among their guests, so confirm the policy for your room.
- **Self-catering.** Torrisdale's castle unit is an apartment, not a hotel room.
