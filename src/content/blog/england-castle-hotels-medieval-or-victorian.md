England's castle stays cover nine centuries. A few put you in walls built in the Norman or medieval period, some in Tudor or Elizabethan forts, and others in Victorian houses built in the style of a medieval fortress. In many of them only part of the hotel is in the castle, with the rest in a lodge, annex or newer building, so the room you book matters as much as the name.

This guide sorts eleven English castle stays by what each says about its own building, using the hotels' own pages and the sources recorded on our hotel pages. Where a hotel does not say which rooms are in the castle, or where the medieval claim could not be confirmed, we say so.

## Why England suits a castle stay

The eleven stays are spread from the Isles of Scilly to Northumberland and Cumbria, so a castle night fits into most English trips. Amberley Castle lies on the South Downs, Langley Castle is a base for Hadrian's Wall visitors, Peckforton is in Cheshire and Augill Castle sits between the Lake District and the Yorkshire Dales. Amberley Castle is listed at about $350 to $750 a night, while St Briavels Castle is a hostel, not a hotel.

## Medieval and fortified: rooms in the old castle

### [Amberley Castle](/hotels/amberley-castle-england/): Amberley, West Sussex

A walled medieval castle with a portcullis at the entrance, a 60-foot curtain wall and a twin-tower gatehouse, in 900-year-old grounds on the South Downs. Its 19 bedrooms are spread across the estate, from the Manor House and Tower House Mews to the Gatehouse Suites in the 17th-century Bishopric Dairy just beyond the walls. Rates run roughly $350 to $750 a night.

**Suits:** couples celebrating, history enthusiasts and weekend escapes from London. **Check:** rooms vary in character and location, so ask where yours is; the page for this hotel notes that stone walls and small windows can make some rooms dark.

### [Langley Castle Hotel](/hotels/langley-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Hexham, Northumberland

A 14th-century castle, first built in 1350, Grade I listed, with walls up to seven feet thick. Nine individually styled rooms are in the main castle, some with four-poster beds, spa baths or saunas; a further 19 dog-friendly rooms are in Castle View, a short walk away, and there is a self-catering villa. A battlements tour runs daily at 10:15am.

**Suits:** couples, Hadrian's Wall visitors and history lovers. **Check:** only nine of 28 rooms are in the castle, so ask for a castle room.

### [Hazlewood Castle & Spa](/hotels/hazlewood-castle-and-spa-united-kingdom/): Tadcaster, North Yorkshire

A castle that goes back to the Domesday Book, described as one of the oldest fortified houses in Yorkshire, with 32 rooms and suites in three buildings: nine in the main castle, 12 in St Margaret's around the courtyard and 11 in St Leonard's. There is a thermal spa with a Finnish sauna and aroma steam room, and 77 acres of woodland.

**Suits:** couples, spa breaks and weddings. **Check:** most rooms are not in the castle itself.

### [Walworth Castle Hotel](/hotels/walworth-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): Darlington, County Durham

A castle of 12th-century origins, completed around 1600, with castle rooms inside the main castle and four-poster suites, some in the towers. The King James Suite is where King James VI of Scotland stayed on 14 April 1603. Annex rooms are in an adjoining modern building, and there is a gatehouse lodge and a holiday cottage.

**Suits:** couples, weddings and heritage lovers. **Check:** book a castle room or tower suite rather than an annex room if the building matters.

### [Star Castle Hotel](/hotels/star-castle-hotel-united-kingdom/): St Mary's, Isles of Scilly

A Grade I fortress built in 1593 under Elizabeth I, with 38 bedrooms in total. Castle Rooms, Superior Castle Rooms, a single and three Guard Rooms on the ramparts are in the fortress; Garden Rooms and Suites are separate, newer cottage-style rooms in the grounds. There is an indoor heated pool and courtesy transport from the airport.

**Suits:** couples, island getaways and history lovers. **Check:** this is an Elizabethan fortress, not a medieval castle, and the garden rooms are not in it.

### [YHA St Briavels Castle](/hotels/yha-st-briavels-castle-united-kingdom/): St Briavels, Forest of Dean

A Norman castle begun between 1075 and 1129, Grade I listed and once a hunting lodge of King John, run as a hostel since 1948 in a building owned by English Heritage. Eight bedrooms sleep about 64 in total, three of them in the east tower. Beds are in shared rooms, private rooms or the whole castle can be booked.

**Suits:** families, groups, budget travellers and walkers in the Wye Valley. **Check:** this is a hostel, not a hotel, and many beds are bunks in shared rooms.

## A Tudor castle

### [Scargill Castle](/hotels/scargill-castle-united-kingdom/): Teesdale, County Durham

A castle built in 1550 on the site of an earlier medieval castle and restored from a crumbling ruin, with a surviving three-storey gatehouse. The stay is one self-catering retreat for two inside the castle, with a banqueting dining hall, living room, bedroom and its own courtyard garden. A welcome hamper of food and wine and a tea tray are provided, and the site was partially excavated by Channel 4's Time Team in 2009.

**Suits:** couples, honeymoons and anniversaries. **Check:** it is a single self-catering unit, so there is no hotel service.

## Built to look medieval

### [Peckforton Castle](/hotels/peckforton-castle-united-kingdom/): Cheshire

A Grade I listed castle built in 1851 in the style of a medieval fortress, with 48 individually appointed bedrooms and suites, including Tower Suites, in a 48-acre estate. The Lodge and The Retreat are separate group-accommodation houses on the estate.

**Suits:** couples, families and groups. **Check:** this is a Victorian castle, not a medieval one.

### [Augill Castle](/hotels/augill-castle-united-kingdom/): Kirkby Stephen, Cumbria

A 19th-century castle between the Lake District and the Yorkshire Dales, with an informal, family-friendly atmosphere and log fires. Castle rooms, most with four-poster beds, are in the castle itself; the hotel also lets Garden Suites, Family Estate Houses and a Stable House outside the main building. It stands at the geographical centre of the British Isles.

**Suits:** families, couples and groups of friends. **Check:** ask for a castle room if you want to be in the main building.

### [Banwell Castle](/hotels/banwell-castle-united-kingdom/): Banwell, Somerset

A Grade II* listed Gothic Revival castle, a mansion completed by 1847, so Victorian rather than medieval. It is still a family home that takes bed-and-breakfast guests in the main house, with 25 acres of grounds and views over the Loxton Valley to the Mendip Hills.

**Suits:** couples and romantic breaks. **Check:** it is a family home with bed and breakfast, not a full-service hotel. Our editorial score for it is the lowest in this guide.

## Not confirmed

### [Otterburn Castle Country House Hotel](/hotels/otterburn-castle-country-house-hotel-united-kingdom/): Otterburn, Northumberland

A Grade II listed castle with 17 en-suite bedrooms in a deer park and woodland of 32 acres, close to the site of the 1388 Battle of Otterburn. The heritage record describes a castellated house rebuilt in 1830 on the site of a 13th-century pele tower, and sources disagree on how much medieval fabric survives. The official site was not accessible when we checked, so we could not confirm the details with the hotel.

**Suits:** couples, weddings and history lovers. **Check:** confirm the age of your building and your room type directly with the hotel.

## What to check before you book

- **The room, not the castle name.** At Langley, Hazlewood, Walworth, Star Castle and Augill, only some rooms are in the castle.
- **Victorian or medieval.** Peckforton, Augill and Banwell are 19th-century buildings.
- **Hostel or self-catering.** St Briavels is a hostel with shared rooms, and Scargill is a self-catering retreat for two.
- **Dogs.** Langley lists 19 dog-friendly rooms in Castle View; ask before bringing a dog elsewhere.
- **Gaps in the facts.** For Otterburn and Banwell we have the least confirmed information, so contact the hotel with any specific question.
