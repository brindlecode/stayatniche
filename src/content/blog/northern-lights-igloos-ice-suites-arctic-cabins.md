Plenty of hotels in the far north mention the northern lights. They are not all the same kind of stay. In a glass igloo you can lie in bed under a glass roof. In an ice hotel you sleep in a room made of ice and snow and go outside to look up. In an Arctic cabin the sky is just outside the door. Which of these you want decides where to book.

This guide compares eight stays in Finland, Sweden and Norway by what you sleep in and what each says about itself. Nothing here promises a sighting: the aurora depends on solar activity and weather, and the Kakslauttanen page says so in as many words.

## What suits the northern lights

The properties below are all in Lapland or northern Norway. Kakslauttanen is at 68 degrees north in Finnish Lapland, Manshausen is above the Arctic Circle at 68°N, Snowhotel Kirkenes is at 69 degrees north and Sorrisniva is at 70 degrees north, in what its page calls the prime aurora zone. For Kakslauttanen, the page points to a remote location and genuinely dark skies.

Timing matters too. Winter is the season for the northern lights: Kakslauttanen's page gives November to March, and its glass igloos run from late August to April. In summer the same places offer the midnight sun instead.

## Sleep under glass

### [Kakslauttanen Arctic Resort](/hotels/kakslauttanen-finland/): Saariselkä, Finnish Lapland

Small, large and Kelo-Glass igloos, the last with log-chalet bedrooms, stand in the resort's East and West villages. The thermal glass stays clear in temperatures down to -30°C, and beds in the igloos sit under a glass roof; the small igloos have two single beds. The resort has a smoke sauna and offers reindeer safaris and husky sledding. Rates run roughly $400 to $900 a night.

**Suits:** couples, aurora hunters and night-sky photographers. **Check:** only the glass igloos give the sky view, and the resort also has ordinary log cabins, suites and snow igloos. Peak season, December to March, books out many months ahead. The igloos are compact, and Ivalo airport is 35 km away, with connecting flights through Helsinki.

### [Arctic Guesthouse & Igloos](/hotels/arctic-guesthouse-and-igloos-finland/): Ranua, Lapland

Ten compact glass igloos on the shore of Lake Ranua, called AuroraHuts, ideally for two people, with Wi-Fi in every igloo. In summer some of them float and move under an electric engine. The property also has ordinary guesthouse rooms and Aurora Lake Lodges, a free electric sauna for guests, and a restaurant open every day of the year.

**Suits:** couples and groups who want northern lights viewing. **Check:** only the igloos are the glass stay, so choose an AuroraHut rather than a guesthouse room.

## Sleep in ice

### [ICEHOTEL 365](/hotels/icehotel-sweden/): Jukkasjärvi, Swedish Lapland

The original ice hotel, open every year since 1989, rebuilt each winter by international artists in ice and snow, with the winter hotel running from December to April. ICEHOTEL 365 stays open year-round with 18 art and deluxe suites at -5°C, cooled by solar energy. Warm rooms and cabins in separate buildings are ordinary rooms. Rates run roughly $500 to $1,800 a night.

**Suits:** design and art lovers, aurora hunters and honeymooners. **Check:** sleeping in the ice rooms is genuinely cold, art suites cost more than the warm rooms, and the stay is remote, with Kiruna airport 17 km away.

### [Sorrisniva Igloo Hotel](/hotels/sorrisniva-igloo-hotel-norway/): Alta, Finnmark

A hotel of ice and snow rebuilt every winter and open from 20 December to 31 March, on the Alta River at 70 degrees north. There are more than 20 ice rooms and suites, an ice chapel and ice sculptures, with igloo rooms, family rooms and themed suites. The separate Arctic Wilderness Lodge has ordinary warm rooms. Rates run roughly $350 to $800 a night.

**Suits:** aurora hunters and adventure couples. **Check:** the ice hotel is open only from 20 December to 31 March; outside that window book the lodge instead.

### [Snowhotel Kirkenes](/hotels/snowhotel-kirkenes-norway/): Kirkenes, Finnmark

Hand-carved snow-and-ice suites made by international ice sculptors, no two alike, kept frozen year-round as Snowhotel 365 at Snow Resort Kirkenes. The resort also has warm Gamme cabins as ordinary accommodation, and offers a king crab fishing safari on the Varangerfjord and husky sledding. Rates run roughly $300 to $700 a night.

**Suits:** adventure couples, foodies and dog-sled fans. **Check:** the warm cabins are not ice rooms, so choose a Snowhotel suite for the ice stay.

## Sleep in an Arctic cabin

### [Treehotel](/hotels/treehotel-sweden/): Harads, Swedish Lapland

Nine separate designer treerooms in the pines, including the Mirrorcube, the UFO and the Bird's Nest, with northern lights viewing in winter and dog sledding and snowshoeing nearby. The separate Guesthouse, with six heritage rooms, is not a treehouse. Rates run roughly $300 to $600 a night.

**Suits:** couples, architecture lovers and design enthusiasts. **Check:** the page lists northern lights viewing in winter, not a sky view from every room, so ask what each treeroom offers.

### [Arctic Bath](/hotels/arctic-bath-sweden/): Harads, Swedish Lapland

A hotel that floats on the Lule River in summer and is frozen into the ice in winter, with 12 cabins: six Floating Cabins reached by a footpath over the water, three Land Suites and three Land Family Cabins. The spa has two dry saunas, a steam sauna, two outdoor Jacuzzis and a cold bath. Rates run roughly $500 to $1,200 a night.

**Suits:** wellness seekers, couples and aurora hunters. **Check:** only half the cabins float; the other six are on land.

### [Manshausen Island Resort](/hotels/manshausen-island-resort-norway/): Steigen archipelago, Nordland

Sea cabins on a private island, the older ones resting on old stone quays and the newer ones out over the sea, plus two Towers on the pier edge. The resort lists northern lights reflecting in the water, kayaking and diving directly from the cabin, and midnight sun swims in summer. Rates run roughly $350 to $700 a night.

**Suits:** adventure couples, kayakers, divers and aurora hunters. **Check:** the stay is an island cabin, not a glass roof, and the aurora depends on the sky.

## What to check before you book

- **No guarantee.** The lights depend on solar activity and weather, so allow several nights rather than one.
- **The room, not the resort.** Kakslauttanen, Arctic Guesthouse, ICEHOTEL, Sorrisniva, Snowhotel and Treehotel all have ordinary rooms or cabins beside the headline stay.
- **Opening dates.** Sorrisniva's ice hotel is open only from 20 December to 31 March, and ICEHOTEL's winter hotel from December to April.
- **Cold.** Ice rooms are genuinely cold; ICEHOTEL's warm rooms and cabins in separate buildings are the alternative.
- **Getting there.** Remote stays need flights and a transfer: Kiruna airport is 17 km from ICEHOTEL and Ivalo airport is 35 km from Kakslauttanen.
