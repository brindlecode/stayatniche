Majakkahotelli is a lighthouse hotel on an island at the edge of the open sea. Every one of its guest rooms is inside the lighthouse tower, decorated in a simple, comfortable maritime style. Beyond the tower, the island has a restaurant, a guest harbour and eight two-person cottages.

## The rooms

The hotel has 15 rooms: seven with a double bed and eight with two separate beds. All of them are in the tower, and all have a sea view except the Economy rooms, which look out on the lighthouse terrace.

- **Economy**: single and double versions, both on the first floor, with a shared toilet and bathroom.
- **Twin**: two separate beds, on floors one to four, with a shared toilet and bathroom.
- **Superior**: a double bed, bathrobes and a radio, on floors five to seven, with a shared toilet and bathroom.
- **Aava Suite**: a double bed and a sofa bed, with windows facing all four compass points and a viewing balcony one floor higher. It has its own toilet and shower. There is no lift, and pets are not allowed in the suite.

Toilets and showers for the shared-bathroom rooms are on the landings. Breakfast can be added when booking. Three rooms can accommodate a dog for an extra fee: two Twin rooms and one Superior room.

## Saunas and tubs

The lighthouse has two saunas that can be booked privately, and by the shore there are hot tubs and a steam barrel. Guests can also use the public sauna sessions, with separate turns for men and women, or book a private session in the lighthouse sauna or the beach sauna on arrival.

## Getting there

The hotel is on an island, so a boat trip has to be booked as well as the room. The lighthouse restaurant also works as a venue for parties and meetings.
