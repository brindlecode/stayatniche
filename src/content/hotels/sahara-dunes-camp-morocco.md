Sahara Dunes Camp is a small, family-run camp built at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes, just outside Merzouga in southern Morocco. It offers a handful of handcrafted Berber tents, and it keeps the number deliberately low so that each guest gets real attention. The dunes here rise to over 150 metres and change colour through the day, from pale gold to deep amber.

## The tents

Every tent is furnished with handwoven Moroccan rugs, carved lanterns and traditional textiles, and each has a real bed with quality linens. Every tent also has a private en-suite bathroom with a hot shower, flush toilet and sink, plus electricity and free Wi-Fi. The camp's staff are on hand around the clock.

Two tent types are featured: a Luxury Double Occupancy Tent for two guests, with views of the dunes, and a Luxury Triple Separate Desert Tent with three separate beds for up to three guests. The camp says it has more configurations on request, from romantic doubles to family tents for four and larger tents for five or more guests, in both shared-bed and separate-bed arrangements.

## What is included

Stays are all-inclusive. Each rate covers a traditional Moroccan dinner and breakfast, mint tea, a sunset camel trek to camp on arrival (or a 4x4 transfer), and an evening of campfire and Berber music. Sandboarding is part of the stay too. Meals are cooked by a kitchen team that makes tagines and harira.

Everyone who works at the camp comes from the region, including the guides. The camp says it runs on solar power, conserves water and aims for tourism to benefit local families from Tessardmin, Hassilbid and Rissani.

## Getting there

The camp offers private pickups from Errachidia and Ouarzazate airports, with longer transfers and tours that include sightseeing on the way, such as the Ziz Valley or the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs. Transfers from Errachidia, Rissani or Merzouga can be added to a booking. Dietary arrangements such as vegan and gluten-free can be requested, and quad biking, 4x4 desert tours and private photography sessions are optional extras.

When you book, say what size of group you are travelling with and which tent arrangement you would like; the camp replies with a personalised quote.
