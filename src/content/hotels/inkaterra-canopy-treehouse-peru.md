The Peruvian Amazon around Puerto Maldonado, specifically the Tambopata National Reserve and its surrounding protected area, is one of the highest-biodiversity ecosystems ever recorded. A single hectare of forest here may contain more species of ant than the entirety of the British Isles contains of all insects combined; a single day's birding can yield 200 species; a night walk reveals a density of amphibian, reptile, and arthropod life that makes the darkness feel inhabited in ways no temperate landscape can approach. Sleeping in the canopy of this forest, at the level where the light penetrates and the most productive layer of the ecosystem concentrates, is an experience measured in species per hour.

Inkaterra's Canopy Tree House is booked as a supplement night on top of a stay at Inkaterra Reserva Amazónica, an eco-luxury lodge with 35 wooden cabañas. After dinner at the main lodge, guests meet a private Explorer Guide and take a short boat ride to the Inkaterra Canopy Walkway, a network of treetop paths linked by suspension bridges. The Canopy Tree House stands on the most remote platform of the walkway, about 28 metres (92 feet) above the forest floor, and from there guests can observe wildlife that is rarely seen from the ground.

The tree house is basic: two beds, a lavatory, a portable toilet, and windows with removable mosquito netting, from which the forest can be watched at dawn. Dawn is the occasion guests rise early for. The shift from forest night to forest day is one of the great acoustic experiences in nature travel, as thousands of birds and amphibians change register simultaneously and the light moves from grey through green to gold. Macaws, toucans, and tanagers pass at eye level or above; howler monkeys declare territories from adjacent trees; and on clear mornings the mist rises through the canopy in layers.

Inkaterra Reserva Amazónica offers à-la-carte excursions, including a twilight river boat ride in search of nightjars, owls, capybaras and caimans, a night walk through the rainforest, a rainforest garden walk and a visit to the palmetum. The Canopy Tree House supplement includes a sunset cocktail, dinner in your room, breakfast and a private night walk across the hanging bridges of the walkway.
