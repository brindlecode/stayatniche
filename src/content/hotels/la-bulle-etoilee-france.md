La Bulle Etoilée is a small bubble hotel in the countryside at Bonnemaison, in the Calvados department of Normandy, about 20 minutes from Caen. Its owners, Manu and Chrysoline, have run it since 1 March 2016 and describe the idea as a childhood dream. The result is a handful of transparent bubbles meant to combine charm, comfort and privacy for a night away from everything.

## The bubbles

There are five bubbles, each with its own name: Zozo, Capu, Camille, Caramel and Groseille. Every one is isolated from the others, set in open countryside with no one overlooking it. Each bubble sleeps a maximum of two people. There are no family accommodations, which makes the place a natural choice for couples.

Each bubble comes with a private hot tub, and breakfast is included in the nightly rate. Bed linen, bath towels and bathrobes are provided. Pets are not allowed on the estate.

## Arrival and departure

Guests are welcomed at reception between 17:00 and 19:30, and the estate can be used until 10:30 on the day of departure. Reservations can be made online from the pages of each bubble, or by calling the owners. Dates can be changed or cancelled under the terms and conditions, and gift cards are available.

## Food and wellbeing

For the evening, the owners offer meal baskets to eat in the bubble, with a seasonal menu they prepare themselves. Meal options do not have to be added when booking, and the choice can be sent up to 72 hours before the stay. Since the start of 2023, massage treatments have also been offered in a small chalet in the middle of the countryside, carried out by a qualified wellness masseuse. These are best reserved early to secure a time slot.

The address is Le Haut Bosq, 14260 Bonnemaison. Ask the owners which of the five bubbles suits you before booking.
