Treehouse Point is a bed and breakfast of seven treehouses in an old-growth forest on the banks of a spring-fed river in Washington's Snoqualmie Valley. The treehouses are built in and around living trees, which the owners say shaped each build. The final treehouse, Ananda, was completed in 2021.

## The treehouses

Each treehouse has its own character. The Temple of the Blue Moon, the first on the property, was designed by Pete Nelson with the classic lines of the Parthenon in mind. A bridge crosses a slope to its door, and inside are a queen bed, a writing desk, reading chairs and a private balcony over the river.

Trillium roosts sixteen feet up in a cedar, with a sitting room below and a sleeping loft reached by a ship's ladder. Burl curls around the trunk of a fir that rises nearly 200 feet, and is reached by a long bridge. Bonbibi is secured to a Western red cedar and climbs by a curved staircase with cedar branch railings; it has no toilet, and guests walk to the bathhouse. Nest is the smallest at 100 square feet, tucked into cedar and spruce, with a deck facing the ponds. Upper Pond is 170 square feet with two lofted single beds and a queen, and sleeps four.

Ananda is supported by two Douglas firs and has floor-to-ceiling windows over the Raging River, a king bed and a gas fireplace. The site calls it the first ADA-accessible commercial treehouse.

## Beyond the treehouses

Three further options sit alongside them. The Cedar Suite is a room on the second floor of the Lodge, with a king bed, a vintage soaking tub and a balcony over the lawn and river. The Hideaway is a cedar cabin with a king bed and a covered porch, with a separate bathroom. The LoveBomb is an upcycled army truck lined with cedar, with a queen bed and a covered deck; the bathhouse is a short walk away.

## Practical notes

Overnight stays are for guests aged 16 and over. Check-ins on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays require a two-night minimum, and Sunday check-ins are for a single night. Weekday reservations open on the first Monday of each month at 2 PM Pacific for the same month of the following year; weekend stays open 60 days ahead. Toilet arrangements differ between treehouses, so check the details of the one you want before booking.
