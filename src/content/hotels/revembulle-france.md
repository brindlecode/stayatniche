Rêvembulle offers a night in a transparent bubble suspended up to 3 m above the ground, in the heart of the Massif des Écrins. The postal address is Valgau Aventure in La Chapelle-en-Valgaudémar, and the idea is simple: sleep under the open sky in the middle of a national park, without the usual drawbacks of camping out.

## The bubble

The bubble is clear, so the night sky is the view. The owners describe a night between sky and earth, with guests settled comfortably inside, at a height of up to 3 m above the ground. They present it as a stay for a weekend for two, a family experience, or a night with friends, and as a way to spend a night in harmony with nature in complete privacy.

## The setting

The site lies in the Écrins national park, in Valgaudémar. The soundtrack is natural: waterfalls and the song of animals. The stay is private.

## Forest massages

Alongside the night in the bubble, the site offers massages in the forest. They take place in a covered, heated space, surrounded by the forest and its natural sounds and music.

## Season and booking

The bubble is seasonal. The most recent season ran from 9 May to 5 October 2026. Reservations are taken directly by email or phone, and the site also sells handmade gift vouchers. Ask the owners about dates for the coming season.
