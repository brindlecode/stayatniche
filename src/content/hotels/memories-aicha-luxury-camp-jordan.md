Memories Aicha Luxury Camp is a camp of 55 tents in the heart of Wadi Rum, tucked into a red valley between rugged mountains and open Arabian desert. The camp's own description is that the site looks more like a Mars colony than a campsite. The idea is to wake up in the wild without giving up hotel-level comfort.

## The tents

Each tent is furnished in yellows, oranges and reds inspired by the colours of the desert sands, and the tents have air conditioning, showers and running water. The transparent roofs let guests watch thousands of stars from bed at night, and by day a private deck looks out over the red landscape, furnished in an oriental style.

There are four categories:

- **Junior Luxury Tent** (375 sq ft): a king bed or two twin beds, for two guests.
- **Panoramic Luxury Tent** (340 sq ft): one king bed, built with stargazing in mind, for two guests. It comes with Dead Sea toiletries.
- **Executive Luxury Tent** (540 sq ft): a king bed and a sofa bed, with a living room, for up to three guests.
- **Panoramic Luxury Suite**: offers extra size and a bed from which to gaze at the moon and stars.

Tents come with an en-suite bathroom and shower, slippers, bathrobes, bottled mineral water, adapters and a hair dryer. The camp is smoke-free, and pets are not allowed.

## Food and facilities

Breakfast is continental, and dinner offers a variety of local dishes prepared by the camp's chefs from fresh local ingredients. The camp also has a library, a terrace, a restaurant and an outdoor barbecue area. A bar under the rock serves non-alcoholic drinks and shisha. Self-parking is available on site.

## Wadi Rum

The camp is a five-minute drive from the Wadi Rum visitors centre. Days can be spent on a jeep tour of the red desert or at a traditional Bedouin dinner cooked under the earth, and the clear night skies are good for stargazing. The camp calls itself family friendly and a good hub for children to have a desert experience.

Check-in is after 14:00 and check-out is at 11:00.
