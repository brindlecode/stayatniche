Belle Tout is a lighthouse bed and breakfast on the cliffs of Beachy Head, near Eastbourne in East Sussex. The lighthouse was built in 1832 and has been restored, and it looks out over the English Channel, the Seven Sisters and the rolling countryside of the South Downs. Only six rooms are offered, and all of them are en-suite.

## Which rooms are in the lighthouse

Not every room is inside the lighthouse itself. The Keeper's Loft is the only bedroom in the original granite lighthouse. It is the lighthouse keeper's original sleeping quarters, a very small room with a double bed on a mezzanine. It is popular, but it does not suit everyone: access is by a steep ladder and the ceiling above the bed is low.

The other five rooms are:

- **Beachy Head**: double bedroom with en-suite shower room.
- **Captain's Cabin**: double bedroom with en-suite shower room.
- **Seven Sisters**: double bedroom with en-suite shower room.
- **South Downs**: the one twin bedroom, which can also be set up as a super-king, with en-suite shower room.
- **Old England**: double bedroom, the only room with a bath in its en-suite.

Every bedroom has tea and coffee making facilities, a hair dryer and a safe. Rooms look over the sea, the Seven Sisters, Beachy Head or the South Downs.

## Shared spaces

The residents' lounge has a roaring fire in the winter months. The Lantern Room gives a 360-degree panorama across the South Downs, with the Seven Sisters in one direction and the Beachy Head lighthouse in the other, and it is the place to watch the sunsets. There is also a guests' office with superfast Wi-Fi for remote work, and a fridge for your own drinks. The lounge, the office and the Lantern Room are open to all guests all day. Breakfast is home cooked.

## Around Beachy Head

The Tiger Inn pub is a five-minute drive away. Birling Gap, with its rock pools and a National Trust café, and Cuckmere Haven are nearby places for walks. The South Downs National Park is an International Dark Skies Reserve, and on clear nights from October to March the Milky Way can be seen above the chalk cliffs.

Guests must be 16 or over, pets are not allowed and the building is entirely non-smoking. It closes for a short time over Christmas and New Year. Before booking the Keeper's Loft, read about the ladder and the low ceiling.
