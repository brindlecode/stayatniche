At Kovářská, a small railway station on the ridge of the Ore Mountains, a few retired railway wagons stand on the unused fourth track and now serve as places to sleep. The station is 850 metres above sea level, surrounded by forest, and the owners describe the stay as a dose of railway-station romance in the mountains.

## The wagons

The wagons are named Alois, Fanynka, Lesní (the forest wagon) and Přednostův (the stationmaster's wagon), and the site also lists a fifth stay called Vechtrovna. Each is described as unique in its position, its view and its design. The insulated wagons are used for comfortable living even in the hardest winter.

The interior is treated as a simple studio, designed by architect Štěpán Řehoř with carpenter Stanislav Vaverčák. There is a built-in small bathroom with a hot shower and flush toilet, and a fully equipped kitchen. Large panoramic windows can be opened a little in summer, and in winter they frame the snowy station while a stove burns inside.

## The station behind the stay

The owners bought the stone station building from the railway administration in 2014, when the 19th-century building was threatened with demolition. They cleared hundreds of tonnes of rubble and rubbish and built a new roof, new ceilings and new windows. They bought discarded wagons and converted them so they would no longer have to sleep in cars and tents during the work. Because they spend much of the year working abroad, they rent the wagons out, and the income pays for further reconstruction. Staying here supports that project.

## Getting there and what is nearby

Kovářská is about 10 minutes from the ski slopes at Klínovec and 15 minutes from Boží Dar, and the wagons have parking beside them. A train from Chomutov runs right up to the wagons only at weekends, from the start of April to the end of October. Buses from Klášterec nad Ohří run to the village often; the stop at the Elektropřístroj crossroads is about 1 km from the station. When snow covers the tracks, you can put on cross-country skis and follow the line toward Měděnec or Vejprty.
