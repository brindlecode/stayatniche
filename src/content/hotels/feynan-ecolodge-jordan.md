Feynan Ecolodge is a lodge in the true sense: a built complex of 26 rooms, with courtyards and terraces, standing in the desert landscape of Wadi Feynan. It sits deep inside the mountainous Dana Biosphere Reserve in southern Jordan, at the end of a rugged track.

## A lodge run on sun and candlelight

The lodge is solar powered and candle-lit, and its serene courtyards and terraces are there for unwinding between outings.

## An unusual partnership

Feynan is owned by the Royal Society for the Conservation of Nature, a Jordanian NGO devoted to protecting the country's finest natural landscapes. EcoHotels, a privately owned Jordanian company founded in 2009 and based in Amman, has managed and operated the lodge since taking it over that year. The lodge first opened in 2005. The two organisations describe it as a partnership between the private sector and an NGO, built around conservation, benefits for local communities and responsible travel.

## Bedouin hospitality and open country

The staff are local, and the lodge presents Bedouin hospitality as the heart of a stay. Guests spend their days walking and exploring the untouched outdoors of the reserve, sitting down for sweet tea with the Bedouin who live in the area, and visiting archaeological sites nearby. The lodge also keeps a calendar of activities, so it is worth looking at what is on during your dates before you travel.

## Who it suits

This is a place for travellers who want a stay shaped by landscape and local culture. Expect a remote setting and candle-lit evenings. If a particular activity or walk matters to you, ask the lodge when you book which ones run on the days you will be there.
