Cuevas La Granja is a rural tourism complex of 18 cave houses in Benalúa, in the Guadix region of the province of Granada, an area known for people who live in caves dug into the hillside. Sierra Nevada forms the backdrop, and the complex sits within the Granada Geopark. The hotel describes the clay of the walls as warm and the surroundings as calm.

## The cave houses

Every unit is a cave house, so the whole property fits the category. The one-bedroom suites include Almaraes and Mundo Perdido. The caves have interior fireplaces and heating that can be switched on in the afternoon, so the cool earth interior does not feel cold.

## Grounds and food

The estate covers 25,000 square metres of gardens. There is a swimming pool, open from June to September, a children's playroom, and a restaurant that serves a buffet breakfast.

## The landscape

The Comarca de Guadix is famous for its cave houses, and Cuevas La Granja describes its complex of cave houses as unique in Andalusia. Sierra Nevada closes the view in the distance and the gardens are laid out around the cave houses, and the complex is a quiet rural base for exploring the Guadix region on foot or by car.

## Planning

If you travel in the cooler months, remember that the pool is only open in summer. Ask the hotel which cave type suits your group, from one-bedroom suites up to larger houses, and note that the restaurant serves a varied breakfast buffet to start the day.
