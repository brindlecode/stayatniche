Baumhaushotel Otterndorf is a cluster of ten houses on stilts, set on 1.4 hectares of grounds surrounded by old trees on the North Sea coast of Lower Saxony. To be precise about the build: the ten guest houses are stilt houses standing 4 m above the ground among the trees, not houses built inside a tree. The site lies close to the sea dyke behind which the Elbe flows into the North Sea, and right beside the Otterndorf lakes.

## The houses

Each stilt house is about 30 m² and holds a sleeping area with a double bed, a sofa corner, a dining area, a bathroom and a pantry kitchen. A wood stove, heating, Wi-Fi and a TV are included, and so are bed linen, towels and firewood. Outside, a terrace of about 10 m² sits at 2.50 m with a beach chair. Steel stairs lead up to the houses, and each one stands on its own, so the quiet and the view come with it.

The houses carry bird names: Amsel, Blaumeise, Buntspecht, Zaunkönig, Rotkehlchen, Star, Kleiber, Spatz, Kohlmeise and Fasan. There are two house types with different roofs, and three colour schemes of blue, green and brown-beige. The other houses take up to four people. Fasan is the wheelchair-accessible house and takes up to six. Some houses allow pets and others are pet-free.

## A tree house in a tree

Guests staying in the 4 m houses can also spend a night in a separate tree house built in a tree. It sleeps two, has no electricity and no insulation, and comes with sleeping bags and mats. It costs nothing extra; leave a note when booking or ask at reception.

## The setting

The grounds are car-free, with parking at the entrance. Guests can use the hotel's canoes and jetty on the three connected lakes next door, light a fire at the fire pit, or rest in a hammock in the forest bathing area. Part of the grounds is kept as a retreat for animals that people do not enter, and birdwatching is possible from the houses. The houses are close to the dyke, so the view runs over meadows to passing ships.

## Practical notes

Check-in is between 15:00 and 17:00 and check-out is by 11:00. The address is Westerwischer Weg 1, 21762 Otterndorf, reachable by car or by train to Otterndorf station. The houses have no food or drink supplied; a picnic basket from a local butcher and bakery can be added to a booking. The height and stairs mean the houses are not suited to children under 6.
