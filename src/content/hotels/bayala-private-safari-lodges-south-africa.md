Bayala Private Safari Lodges stand inside Zuka Private Game Reserve, a Big Five reserve in the ancient kingdom of Zululand, about three hours' drive north of Durban. Two neighbouring lodges, Safari Lodge and Founders Lodge, sit within walking distance of one another. They offer the same dining, facilities and safari activities, and the main difference between them is the room category.

## The lodges

Safari Lodge has 24 Classic Rooms with warm, contemporary décor in natural tones. Founders Lodge has 24 Superior Rooms: 10 Superior Rooms and 14 Superior Patio Rooms that look out over the surrounding bush from behind secure fencing. Eight of the patio rooms have interleading doors, which makes four Family Suites for families or groups. Every room has an en-suite bathroom with a walk-in shower, air conditioning, quality linens, tea and coffee-making facilities and a hairdryer. Both lodges have a lounge, a rustic bar, a swimming pool and indoor and outdoor dining areas.

## Food

The kitchens turn out South African dishes such as braais and potjies, along with full English breakfasts and light lunches. Dinner can be taken in the lodge or around the fire in the traditional boma. Private bush breakfasts and bush dinners can be arranged by prior booking, subject to availability.

## On safari

The morning game drive sets off before sunrise. The afternoon drive leaves as the temperature cools, stops for a traditional sundowner and returns after dark, when the guide watches for nocturnal creatures. Guided bush walks are also part of the activities. The reserve is home to elephant, black and white rhino, lion, leopard, buffalo, cheetah, giraffe and zebra, along with many birds. On request, subject to availability and at extra cost, guests can join hands-on conservation work such as rhino dehorning.

## The reserve

Zuka is the cornerstone of a land-assembly project begun in 2001, which has grown into a 10,300-hectare rewilding area. It is one of three founding members of the 29,000-hectare Munywana Conservancy, which shares a boundary with the iSimangaliso Wetland Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The area is malaria-free. From the lodges, guests can reach places including Lake St Lucia, Sodwana Bay, Hluhluwe-iMfolozi Park and uMkhuze Game Reserve.

Arrival is from 14:00 and departure is at 10:00.
