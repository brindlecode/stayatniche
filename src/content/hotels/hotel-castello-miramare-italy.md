Hotel Castello Miramare is a 3-star hotel on the seafront at Pegli, a district a few kilometres from the centre of Genoa. Guests stay in an old castle that has kept its atmosphere and part of its architectural structure, with the beach in front of the door.

## The rooms

Each room has a private bathroom with shower, air conditioning and heating, a flat-screen TV, a minibar, a telephone and a kettle. Most rooms face the sea. Free wireless internet is available in all rooms and common areas.

## The seafront

The hotel faces the beach of Pegli and offers direct access to the public beach, with a beach volleyball court that can be used for a fee. Beach clubs are nearby. A bar, room service and a breakfast room are available, and the hotel has arrangements with local restaurants.

## Getting around

The position suits both long holidays and short city stops. Pegli can be reached by train or bus, and also by sea: the Navebus service connects Pegli with Genoa's Porto Antico, the heart of the city with the Aquarium and the museums, in under 30 minutes. Cristoforo Colombo airport is only 4.5 kilometres away.

## Nearby

Villa Durazzo Pallavicini, a historic noble residence, is close by. For active travellers, there is an adventure park nearby, the E1 walking route and the public swimming pool at Pra. Concerts and events take place a few kilometres away at the RDS Stadium and its Fiumara shopping centre, and the Luigi Ferraris stadium is 15 kilometres away.
