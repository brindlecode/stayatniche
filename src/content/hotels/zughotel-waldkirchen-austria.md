The ZUGHOTEL in Waldkirchen an der Thaya, in Lower Austria, is a hotel made of former ÖBB construction-train wagons. The idea behind it is "second life" and re-use: by giving the wagons a new purpose, the hotel spares the environment and keeps a piece of railway history that would otherwise be lost. Two wagons stand at the station and together sleep eight guests.

## The wagons

Sleeping wagon 1 has four single-bed rooms. Sleeping wagon 2 has one double-bed room and two single-bed rooms. Each wagon is fitted with a small kitchen and seating corner, a bathroom with shower and a separate toilet. Tables and benches stand in front of the wagons for breakfast outdoors or a relaxed evening.

A minibar holds white and red wine, sparkling wine, beer, soft drinks, mineral water, Soletti crackers, peanuts and regional chocolate, in reusable bottles where drinks are concerned. Anything taken from it is added to the bill, which is emailed after the stay.

## Breakfast

Breakfast is brought to your wagon at about 8:00, and a vegetarian version is available if you ask. Regional sourcing matters here: the milk comes from the Exl dairy farm in Kainraths, the baked goods from the Slow Food baker Kasses in Thaya or from the Müssauer confectioner in Waidhofen an der Thaya, and the jams come from the region.

## Arrival

Rooms are available from 16:00 on the day of arrival, and wagons should be vacated by 10:00 on the day of departure. Keys are handed over contactless from key safes on the left side of the station veranda, with the codes sent a few days before arrival. The key also opens the bicycle room in the station building, which has charging points for e-bikes. Free parking is available on the station grounds.

## The surroundings

The region's best-known attraction is the Thayarunde, a 111-kilometre cycling route, 50 kilometres of it on the former Thayatalbahn railway line and the rest on quiet, well-signposted side roads. Themed cycle loops focus on the five senses, and the Knödel-Land cycle path leads to places serving dumpling specialities. Along the Thaya and across the region, river baths and ponds invite a swim, including the river baths in Dobersberg, Thaya and Waidhofen an der Thaya.

The 2026 season ended on 21 September; ask the hotel for the dates of the next season.
