Eight of the ten guest rooms at The Old Railway Station are in restored Pullman railway carriages, and the other two are in the Station House itself. This bed and breakfast sits in the former Petworth railway station in West Sussex, in the South Downs, and every room is en-suite with breakfast included.

## The carriages

The carriage rooms arrived in stages. Two Pullman cars, Mimosa (1914) and Alicante (1912), came to the site in 1998 and were renovated into four guest rooms. Flora (1923) followed a few years later, and Montana, her sister car from the same year, completed the set. Across the four cars there are eight rooms: two large King Pullman rooms with king-size beds and an en-suite with both shower and bath, and six Standard Pullman rooms with double or king-size beds and a shower over the bath. Only two guests can stay in a carriage room.

## The Station House

The two rooms in the station building are the Lower and the Upper. The Lower is one of the larger rooms and has a super king bed that can be set up as twins, a walk-in shower and a mobility access level of 3. The Upper is the largest room, approached by a spiral staircase, and it is the only room where an extra bed can be added to sleep three. The building also holds a self-catering one-bedroom suite, the Station Masters Suite, with a two-night minimum stay.

## Eating and drinking

Full English or continental breakfast is served in the Waiting Room, or on the platform when the weather allows, and a continental breakfast can be brought to your carriage. Afternoon tea is served every day with advance booking, and evening dining takes place in Princess Ena, a Pullman carriage from 1906.

## The surroundings

Chichester is 11 miles away, and Goodwood Racecourse, Motor Circuit and House are 7 to 8 miles away across the South Downs. Midhurst, home of polo at Cowdray Park, and the antique shops of Petworth are also close.

## Good to know

Check-in is from 3pm to 6pm and check-out is by 11am. Children under 10 cannot stay overnight, and dogs cannot stay either, apart from assistance dogs.
