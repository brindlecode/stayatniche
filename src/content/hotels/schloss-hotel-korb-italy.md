Schloss Hotel Korb is a 4-star superior hotel in Missian, in the municipality of Eppan in South Tyrol, with views of Bolzano and the Dolomites. The hotel describes its accommodation as fairy-tale nights in the castle and the residence.

## Castle or residence

The property has two parts, and the difference matters when you choose a room. The castle houses the historic suites and some of the double rooms. The residence is a newer building beside it, accessible by lift, with the panorama suites and exclusive doubles.

In the castle:

- **Castle suite**, about 40 m², for two to four people, combining historic charm with elegant comfort. Not all castle suites can be reached by lift.
- **Terrace suite**, about 35 m², for two or three people, with a generous terrace; accessible only by stairs.
- **Comfort double**, about 20 m², in the castle or the residence, small but charming and simply furnished.

In the residence:

- **Panoramic suite**, about 50 m², with views of Bolzano and the Dolomites.
- **Double room exclusive**, about 35 m², with a private balcony and the same views.
- **South Tyrolean suite**, about 70 m², furnished entirely in Swiss pine, looking over Bolzano and the vineyards.
- **Garden suite**, about 55 m², on the ground floor with a small private garden.

If sleeping in the old walls is the point of your trip, ask for a castle suite or a castle comfort double.

## Facilities

The outdoor and indoor pools are open daily from 8am to 8pm. The gym and the tennis court are free for hotel guests. The hotel also lists a gourmet food and beverage offering and a programme of castle experiences.

## Practical notes

Check-in is from 2:30pm to 7:30pm and check-out is by 11am. Reception is staffed daily from 8am to 8pm. The wrought-iron gate at the entrance is closed in the evening, so guests are asked to carry their room key. By train, travel to Bolzano station and continue to the hotel; Flixbus and South Tyrol buses serve Bolzano from several airports. In Italy driving with lights on and carrying a safety vest are compulsory.
