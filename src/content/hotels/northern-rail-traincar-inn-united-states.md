The Northern Rail Traincar Inn is a hotel built from genuine train boxcars. The cars have been renovated with modern amenities and turned into guest rooms and suites, each with its own private bathroom. It stands at 1730 Highway 3 in Two Harbors, Minnesota, and takes guests all year except April.

## The boxcars

The boxcars are connected by an enclosed hallway that is meant to feel like an old-fashioned station platform. Most rooms are what the inn calls Porter rooms, each occupying half of a boxcar. A Porter room has a queen bed, or two queen beds in the Porter Two version. Some come with a sleeper sofa. Every room has a mini fridge, a 40-inch smart TV with a DVD player, heating and air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private shower bath. Room 106 is the wheelchair-accessible Lighthouse room, with an equipped bathroom.

The Conductor Suites are the largest units. The King Suite is a full boxcar with a little added floor space. It has a king bed in the bedroom, and a living room with an electric fireplace, a queen-size pull-down Murphy bed, a queen sofa sleeper, two wing chairs and a dining table with four chairs. The bathroom has a full tub and shower, and there is a microwave. The two King Suites adjoin, which suits extended family stays.

Each room follows one of several themes: Victorian, Oriental, Safari, Fishing, Lighthouse, Bear or Moose, plus a Rose Room.

## The setting

The inn sits in the woods, with views of the rugged Northwoods from every room. Deer, birds, squirrels and woodpeckers are regular visitors, and with luck guests may spot a bear, an eagle, a moose or a wolf. The depot-style main building serves as the reception and breakfast area, and it is also a place to play games or sit by the fireplace with a book. A continental breakfast is included, and there is a lending library of games, toys and DVDs. A microwave is available in the lobby, and snowshoes can be borrowed in winter.

The inn is smoke-free and pet-free.

## Getting around

Two Harbors is three miles south of the inn, near the junction of Highway 61 and County Highway 3, and Duluth is about 35 minutes away. From here guests can reach the Superior Hiking Trail, state snowmobile and ski trails, Gooseberry Falls, Split Rock Lighthouse and the Gitchi-Gami State Bicycle Trail.

If you have a favourite among the themes, ask for that boxcar when you book.
