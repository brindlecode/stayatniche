The Train Station Inn sits in the village of Tatamagouche, Nova Scotia, where a 600-foot, 400-ton train remains a dominant sight. The guest rooms are vintage Canadian-built cabooses and boxcars, authentic on the outside and redesigned inside as private suites that echo their railway past. The original station building, which dates from around 1887, is part of the property.

## The railway cars

Each car has its own private washroom. Caboose #4 is the orange one directly behind the station. It is coupled between the boxcar named "Jimmie" and the dining car. It has a queen bed plus a bunk bed with two singles, a bathtub, and a maximum occupancy of four. It suits two adults and two children.

Caboose #5 dates from 1912. It sleeps two, with a queen bed on the main level and a lookout in the cupola that makes a reading nook. It also has a bathtub.

Wi-Fi is free throughout the property, with secured and unsecured access points in the lounge car and the cafe, though the inn cannot guarantee a connection inside the cars.

## Eating and drinking

The original station building houses a locally roasted coffee bar and cafe, and a large, eclectic gift shop. The Baggage Room Bar serves local craft beers and ciders. The Railway Dining Car and the Jitney Cafe cook with produce from the inn's own garden, grown without pesticides, including vegetables and edible flowers. In summer the station platform hosts live music two or three nights a week.

## The village

The Trans Canada Trail follows the old rail bed from the station along Tatamagouche Bay, past Creamery Square and Patterson Wharf Park, over Campbell's train bridge and on to Nelson Park. Creamery Square holds a Saturday morning farmers' market, a heritage centre and a performing arts centre. An arts centre next door to the station stages events through the summer. Elsewhere in the village are cafes, bakeries, antique shops, galleries, museums, markets and microbreweries, among them Tata Brew Co. Walking and cycling trails run nearby, and bike rentals are available. A small road train tours the village and the trail.

## Good to know

Check-in is any time after 3 pm, with a self-check-in process for late arrivals that is described in the confirmation email. Pets are not allowed, and neither is smoking or vaping in the accommodations. A weekend rate applies on Friday and Saturday nights, so ask when booking.
