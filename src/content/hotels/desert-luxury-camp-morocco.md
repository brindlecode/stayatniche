Desert Luxury Camp runs two tented camps in the Moroccan Sahara. The Erg Chebbi camp lies in the dunes near Merzouga, and the Erg Chigaga camp is by M'Hamid. It was set up by guides who grew up in the area, with the aim of keeping the stillness of the desert while still offering comfort.

## The tents

Guests sleep in tents fitted with en-suite bathrooms, hot showers, flushing toilets and comfortable beds, and with air conditioning at night. Winters bring cold nights, so the camps heat the tents with electrical or gas heaters, with hot-water bottles or electric blankets on the beds. By late March to early April the tents generally no longer need heating. The camp uses solar power, and the tents are built to withstand heavy winds. One of the restaurants is built of clay bricks and serves as a refuge in bad weather.

## Food and activities

Stays are all-inclusive: meals and cold non-alcoholic drinks are covered, as are many of the activities. There is a kitchen on site and a chef trained in a Marrakech culinary school. Dinner is three courses, such as a tagine, lamb mechoui or seven-vegetable couscous, and is served under the stars or in the dining tent. Breakfast brings jams, cheeses, honey, yoghurt and Moroccan pancakes, and lunch is usually a light picnic. Vegetarian and gluten-free diets can be catered for.

Between meals there are guided camel rides, sandboarding on the dunes, guided walks and visits to nomads, plus handball and table games. For something different, the sand hammam lets guests bury themselves in the sand.

## Planning a visit

The camp is open all year, but from the end of June to August only five tents take bookings. March to May and late September to November are the seasons most guests prefer. Transfers to the camp are by 4x4 with luggage taken directly, and the team can meet guests at Errachidia airport at any time, even late in the evening, with a light dinner on arrival. A stay of two nights is recommended, though not required.
