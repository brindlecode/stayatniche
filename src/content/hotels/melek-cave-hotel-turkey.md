Melek Cave Hotel is in the middle of historic Göreme, surrounded by fairy chimneys. Its website says that every room is carved from the rock and furnished individually, which makes it one of the plainest examples of a whole-property cave hotel in the town.

## The rooms

The site lists six cave room types: a Deluxe Cave Room (35 sq m, garden, mountain and courtyard views, double bed), a Cave Double Room (25 sq m, park view), an Arch Twin Room (25 sq m, mountain view, two single beds), a Double Cave Room (25 sq m, courtyard view, one double and one single bed), a Cave Triple Room (25 sq m, courtyard view, three single beds) and a Cave Family Room (35 sq m, town view, one double and two single beds). Rooms have clean, modern bathrooms and a TV.

## Food and facilities

Breakfast is served free each morning on the terrace. There is a shared lounge for relaxing and meeting other guests, and a garden where you can sit with a drink after a day out. Other listed services include free fast Wi-Fi, a restaurant and car rental through the hotel.

## Getting around

A free transfer is available from the bus station (otogar) to the hotel. The hotel can arrange a balloon flight, and bicycles can be used to explore the area. The Göreme Open Air Museum is a short walk away. The owners are described as welcoming and happy to share local knowledge.

## Practical notes

The listed address is Gafeli Mah., Ünlü Sok. No:28, 50180 Göreme. It suits travellers who want a simple, central cave room rather than a luxury resort.
