Sitka Lighthouse stands on a private one-acre island in Sitka Sound, a five-minute boat ride from the harbour in Sitka, Alaska. Guests stay inside the lighthouse itself, and the island, with its own boat dock, is theirs for the duration.

## A lighthouse with a history

The tower was built in 1983 as Rockwell Lighthouse by Burgess Bauder, a local Sitka veterinarian who wanted a working lighthouse on the rugged shoreline. Because of the island's remoteness, building materials had to be delivered by helicopter. Registered with the U.S. Coast Guard, it long served as a navigational aid and became a familiar marker for mariners and a landmark for the Sitka community. More than forty years later, Teal West took it on as Sitka Lighthouse and set about restoring it.

## Inside

The house has two bedrooms and one and a half baths and sleeps up to six. The queen-size main bedroom is on the second floor. The third floor has four twin beds and a half bath, which suits a family or a group of friends. A glass-walled room looks out over the water, and a wrap-around deck is the place for morning coffee, with views over the Eastern Channel and eagles passing overhead. The kitchen is fully equipped, and the house has WiFi and a Smart TV.

Outside, there is a hot tub, a fire pit and a grill on the deck. Four kayaks are provided for exploring the island's surroundings, and a motorised boat is available for trips into town, although boating experience is required. The coast around the dock has volcanic islands and quiet coves, and whales, sea lions and eagles can be seen from the lighthouse.

## Getting there

Overnight stays include the airport shuttle and a water taxi on arrival and departure, with luggage storage and parking also provided. Groceries can be pre-ordered online before arrival so that they are waiting at check-in. A concierge service arranges fishing charters, boat tours, wine deliveries and dinner reservations. Guests also get a guest guide after booking, plus direct contact with the owner.

Beyond the island, options include a whale watching and wine pairing trip, a fishing excursion, a hike on a nearby trail or a walk through downtown Sitka. Reservations can be made online, by phone or by email, and anyone with accessibility needs should ask before booking.
