Bushman's Desert Camp sits on Farm Cha-re in the Namib Desert, 33 km north of Solitaire on the C14. The farm was once used for Karakul sheep, but the land proved too harsh for farming. Its owners decided it was a good place to introduce guests to the desert instead, since it holds dunes, plains, mountains, canyons and sandstone mountains within a single property.

## Where it came from

The camp grew out of Sossus on Foot, a guiding company founded in 1996. Boesman, who left a career as an insurance broker to get back to nature, began with a transfer service for the final 5 km into Sossusvlei, where 4x4 vehicles are essential, and added guided walks in Sossusvlei in 1999. After a change in government legislation the operation moved out and began offering desert tours on its own farm. Guests on those tours needed somewhere to sleep, so campsites came first and self-catering units followed.

## The units

There are twelve en-suite self-catering units, built around an open-air concept. Each has fully opening sliding doors at the front and large windows along one side, so the room feels part of the landscape. Beds are twins with bedding provided, and triple and double rooms are available. Every unit has a private verandah with barbecue facilities, a shaded carport, a solar-heated hot shower and a 220V power point. The kitchen basics are supplied: plates, cutlery, a kettle, pots and a gas burner.

There is no WiFi. Mobile network (MTC) reception is available at the central reception area. A small swimming pool and a bar are shared by room guests and campers alike. Barbecue packs and firewood can be arranged, along with garden salads and garlic bread for dinner, or coffee and rusks for the morning, so mention meals when booking. The Japanese Kitchen opens for the December holidays and on selected weekends.

## Getting there and what to do

The camp is 113 km from Sesriem, the entrance to Sossusvlei, and the site presents it as a stopover for a Sossusvlei and Deadvlei trip on the C14 between Swakopmund and Windhoek. Look for a signpost with shoes hanging from it, turn off, and the camp is 2 km further in. Other distances are Windhoek 230 km, Walvis Bay 200 km and Swakopmund 230 km.

On the property you can book a guided desert tour, drive 4x4 trails or walk self-guided hiking trails.
