Only the Aqua Villas at Pullman Maldives Maamutaa have an underwater bedroom. The rest of the resort is ordinary beach and overwater villas, which have no submerged room. Anyone booking for the underwater night should choose the Aqua Villa specifically.

## The Aqua Villa

The Aqua Villa has two levels and covers 209 square metres. Its master bedroom is submerged beneath the lagoon, an aquarium-style room that gives an open view of the marine life outside, so guests can wake up to corals and schools of fish. Upstairs, a second bedroom looks across the ocean, and the villa also has a large swimming pool with a private deck.

The villa has indoor and outdoor showers, an island bathtub, a Nespresso machine, a lounge on the balcony, loungers and direct access to the lagoon. The pool faces east, which suits a sunrise dip or yoga session, and the Sol Rising sunrise bar, serving breakfast and fresh juices, is a short walk away.

## The rest of the resort

The resort has 122 villas in total, spread across Maamutaa, a private island with 18 hectares of vegetation. They include beach villas and pool villas among the trees, and overwater villas, among them the Two Bedroom Ocean Pool Villa. The Four Bedroom Ocean Pool Retreat is an overwater villa of 1,474 square metres with a private gym, a spa room, a steam room and one of the largest private overwater infinity pools in the Maldives.

Stays are all-inclusive, with unlimited dining and drinks at the resort's three restaurants and three bars, plus activities and excursions such as watersports and yoga.

## Getting there

The resort is in the Gaafu Alifu Atoll. The journey starts with a 55-minute flight from Malé to Kooddoo Airport, followed by a 15-minute speedboat ride to the island. A private seaplane transfer is the alternative. Guests are welcomed by the resort team, and island hosts give a guided tour on the way to the villa.
