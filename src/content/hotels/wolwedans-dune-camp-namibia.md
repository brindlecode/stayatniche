Dune Camp is the original camp of Wolwedans, set on top of a 250-metre dune in the NamibRand Nature Reserve in Namibia. From up there there are panoramic views across the desert. Canvas tents, shared dinners and sunsets set the pace, and the aim is a laid-back but stylish stay in which guests slow down and talk to one another.

## A camp with a past

Wolwedans began in 1995 as a dream shared by Albi Brückner and his son Stephan, and it started with four igloo tents. Dune Camp stands where the first four Wolwedans Igloo Dome tents were put up. The igloos are long gone, but the camp has tried to keep the down-to-earth spirit of that time while adding comfort and style. It is a place that carries many memories.

## The reserve

Wolwedans sits deep inside the 220,000-hectare NamibRand Nature Reserve, one of the largest private reserves in southern Africa, and describes itself as a Dark Sky reserve. The camp is one of five in the Wolwedans collection, alongside Desert Lodge, Mountain View Suite, Plains Camp and Boulders Camp. Each has its own stretch of the reserve. Desert Lodge has ten tented rooms, and Boulders Camp lies about three hours south of the others.

## What to do

Walking the dunes on foot is part of the offer, and the wider Wolwedans programme includes dune cruising, horse riding and a Heart and Home village tour. Guests are encouraged to go slowly, take in the long views and enjoy the quiet of the desert.

Wolwedans also puts its stays towards conservation of the NamibRand and the development of Namibia and its communities, a commitment it describes as a balance of people, planet and profit.

When you enquire, ask how Dune Camp's tents and layout differ from the neighbouring lodges so you can choose the one that suits your trip.
