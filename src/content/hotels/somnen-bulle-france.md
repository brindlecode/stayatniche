Somn'en Bulle offers nights in translucent inflatable bubbles in a forest on the edge of the Aveyron gorges, in the Occitanie region of southern France. The site is near the village of Najac, one of the villages classed among the most beautiful in France, and the site describes itself as close to Toulouse. Because the bubbles are transparent, they give a view of the surrounding nature and of the night sky.

## The bubbles

Each bubble is modular: an access airlock that serves as a dressing area, a 4-metre sphere for the bedroom and a 2.7-metre sphere for the bathroom. No two bubbles are the same, and each one has its own path and private space, so each stay stays private.

Inside, there are two bedside lamps, a power strip, a fridge, a coffee maker, a kettle and hot water. There is no Wi-Fi. Organic toiletries are provided, and the estate uses dry toilets. A quiet turbine keeps the bubble gently inflated, and a battery-backed system keeps it running in a power cut.

The bubbles can be used in any season. A radiator and a very warm duvet are provided, but the bubble is open-air accommodation and is not insulated, so anyone who feels the cold may prefer the warmer months. There is no air conditioning. The bubbles are waterproof, and sleeping through rain or a storm is described as a pleasure. In severe weather, such as an orange Météo-France warning or winds above 90 km/h, the night can be postponed.

## Onsen-style jacuzzi

A jacuzzi inspired by Japanese onsen has opened in 2026 as an addition to the bubbles, a hot bath in a calm, pared-back setting.

## Around Najac

The estate looks out on the forest, where you can hear birds, wind in the trees and, in the distance, roe deer. The surrounding area offers other villages classed among the most beautiful in France, including Cordes-sur-Ciel and Saint-Antonin-Noble-Val. You can explore the Aveyron gorges by kayak or mountain bike, or fly over Najac in a microlight. Meal baskets with local produce and gift vouchers are available.

Pets are not accepted, smoking is forbidden in the bubbles and parking is at the entrance to the estate.
