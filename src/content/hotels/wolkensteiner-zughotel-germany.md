The Wolkensteiner Zughotel is a hotel built from railway carriages in the small Ore Mountains town of Wolkenstein in Saxony, between Marienberg and Annaberg-Buchholz. It stands at Am Bahnsteig 10, directly on the B101 below Wolkenstein castle in the Zschopau valley. It presents itself as the only train hotel in Germany where you can both sleep and eat traditional Ore Mountains food.

## How the train came together

The train was first put together in 1991 from a Mitropa buffet car built in 1976 and a former wooden hospital wagon from 1928, which was converted into a bar and reception. Over the years more cars were coupled on. Today the hotel includes two sleeping cars fitted out for 20 beds, a former culture car that now serves as the kitchen, a salon car that holds the restaurant, and a former track-gang wagon rebuilt as shared showers and toilets. In all, the hotel offers 54 sleeping places in different classes.

## The rooms

The sleeping-car compartments come in first and second class, with timber panelling and the feel of a long journey. Beyond them the range includes a government compartment and two suites, Erna and Walter. Each suite has two separate beds, a small seating area with a TV, and a bathroom with shower and toilet. The suite rates include breakfast, bed linen, towels and parking. Dogs are not allowed, and the sleeping-car compartments cannot be booked from January to the end of March.

For a longer stay there are five wagons converted into holiday apartments, each with a living and sleeping area, a kitchen and a bathroom. Two more holiday apartments are in the old station building, which dates from 1865 and now contains eleven apartments in all.

## Eating and meeting

The salon car serves breakfast and hearty Saxon dishes, with the food coming from the kitchen wagon. There is also a large beer terrace, and the Café Alter Bahnmeister in the former station building serves coffee, cake and ice-cream sundaes. Groups can meet in the salon car from the former East German government train, which survives in its original condition.

## Who it suits

Its own list of visitors includes cyclists, motorcyclists, hikers, US-car fans, couples and railway enthusiasts among the guests. Rooms are booked through the hotel's enquiry form, so ask about the compartment or wagon you would like when you get in touch.
