The Ostsee Baumhaushotel stands at Alte Landstraße 16 in Wangels, in the Ostholstein district of Schleswig-Holstein. It describes itself as the northernmost treehouse hotel in Germany. The site lies in the Bungsberg landscape protection area, near the Baltic Sea, Weißenhäuser Strand and the Weissenhaus Resort.

## The treehouses

There are seven treehouse lodges and seven treehouse lofts. They are free-standing houses built on stilts, standing about five metres above the ground rather than in a tree. The hotel stresses that they are not classic rustic huts and not classic hotel rooms. They are modern, sustainably built houses at the height of the treetops, in clean Nordic-style design with a lot of wood and generous terraces. Each is heated and air-conditioned and usable all year round.

The two types differ in character. The Baumhaus Loft is a design treehouse with a panoramic view, and the Baumhaus Lodge is a cosy lodge aimed at a close-to-nature stay.

The houses are spaced well apart, between meadows, trees and the neighbouring alder carr woodland. This gives each guest a private retreat and their own terrace, and the setting looks out over the gentle hills of East Holstein.

## Spa and food

Every booking includes the nature spa, which has a sauna, an outdoor whirlpool and a rest area. Wi-Fi, freshly made beds and towels are included as well. Breakfast baskets, picnics and small extras for special occasions can be added. The family has owned the land for generations. It began as a farm, then became a popular restaurant, and is now the treehouse hotel.

## Who it suits

The concept is aimed deliberately at adults who value quiet and privacy, whether as a couple or alone. Stays start at two nights, a rule the hotel sets so guests can properly arrive. Its themed breaks include a romantic stay for two, a break for friends and a solo "Me Time" stay.

## Around the area

A nature trail runs through the grounds. The Baltic coast is only a few kilometres away, so a walk by the water or a trip to Heiligenhafen or Weißenhäuser Strand is easy to fit in. The surroundings also suit exploring by bike. In autumn, fruit such as apples, blackberries and plums on the grounds may be picked by guests.
