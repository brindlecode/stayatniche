Four metres below the surface of the Indian Ocean, with the reef life of Pemba Channel drifting past the windows in the dark, conventional hotel categories stop being useful. The Manta Resort's Underwater Room is something separate: a submerged bedroom off remote Pemba Island, in the northern Zanzibar Archipelago.

The three-level suite is anchored about 250 metres off the shore of Pemba Island, floating above a coral reef garden in the protected Pemba Channel. The top level is a rooftop sunbathing deck, open to the stars at night; the middle level is a shaded lounge with an eco-designed bathroom; and the double bedroom sits four metres below the sea surface.

**The Underwater Bedroom**

Nearly 360 degrees of glass surround the double bed. The glass is not there to frame the ocean in the way a hotel window frames a city — it places you inside it. In daylight, schools of parrotfish, trumpetfish and groupers pass the windows, with the occasional octopus or squid. At night, soft underwater lighting attracts nocturnal sea creatures to the glass.

Most guests report being entirely unable to sleep. Not from discomfort. From an unwillingness to close their eyes.

**The Structure**

The rooftop deck is a sunbathing platform by day and, at night, an open-air seat under a sky the resort describes as free from light pollution.

There is no live-aboard host. The resort delivers curated meals by boat, and the suite comes with a stocked bar fridge, snorkelling gear, a mobile phone to reach resort staff and a personal concierge team. The Underwater Room is designed for two guests.

**Pemba Island**

The island itself is worth the journey on its own terms. The Pemba Channel drops to considerable depth just offshore, generating upwellings that sustain some of the healthiest coral in the Indian Ocean. Tourist infrastructure is minimal — a handful of lodges, no resort strip — and the villages of clove farmers and fishermen along the coast operate at a pace that feels genuinely unhurried.

The Manta Resort sits at Pemba's northern tip, about 1.5 hours by road from Pemba Airport, and handles the boat logistics for reaching the Underwater Room. According to the resort's FAQ, there is a three-night minimum stay, and the rate covers accommodation, all meals and drinks, wellness treatments and laundry; airport transfers and guided excursions, including diving and snorkelling trips, cost extra.
