Robins Nest is a treehouse hotel near Witzenhausen in northern Hesse, close to Schloss Berlepsch. Its founder, Peter Becker, started with a tipi, and the first treehouses opened in the summer of 2015. He builds with high-quality wood and recycled materials, and the site's own plan is for a small village of tree nests joined by bridges and tunnels.

## The treehouses

The nests sit in the treetops at around seven metres. Each one is reached by stairs and suspension bridges. The range includes a birch treehouse, a cork treehouse, a trunk house and a ball-shaped treehouse, each sleeping two to four people or, in the ball's case, two. There is also a Wald Loft and a pair of domes, both for two to four guests.

The stilt house is a separate building raised on stilts, and it sleeps two to eight people. It has its own bathroom with shower and toilet, as does the Wald Loft. In the treehouses, washing is done with a basin and a water container, and the four-person treehouses have dry composting toilets. Each dome has an outdoor bathing hut with a toilet and shower, usable from April to October. A washroom with a shower near the restaurant toilets is open round the clock.

Tree tents are also on offer. They can be booked by adults only, and dogs are not allowed in them or in the domes. Dogs can stay elsewhere on the site, but two wobbly bridges lead to the trunk house, which some dogs dislike.

## Food, sauna and practicalities

A breakfast buffet is included in the stay, along with parking on the grounds, towels, bed linen and the final clean. Dinner is served in the restaurant, whose opening depends on the season. The sauna can be used all year, and bathrobes and sauna towels can be hired at the restaurant. Barbecuing on the grounds is not allowed.

Restaurant staff welcome guests from 15:00 and take back the treehouse key by 11:00. Single nights can be booked on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. Arrivals and departures are not possible on Saturdays and Mondays.

Children can stay in the high beds, which have fall protection. Child stays are not possible from November to March or in September, which the hotel keeps for couples.

## Getting there and nearby

The easiest way to arrive is by car, and a large car park is on site. By train, the nearest station is in Gertenbach, about 4 km away, or a 45-minute walk. Schloss Berlepsch is a short distance away, and the hotel's site has a page on hiking in the area.
