Whitepod sits above the village of Les Cerniers, with its pods at 1,400 metres in the Valais Alps, and the founding premise — that an architecturally inventive small-footprint accommodation could offer a more genuine Alpine experience than a conventional ski hotel — has held up over two decades of operation. The geodesic domes each have a large bay window that frames the alpine landscape, a pellet stove, a private bathroom and a panoramic terrace. The pods are reached on foot, a walk of about 15 minutes from reception and parking in summer and about 20 minutes in winter.

The central chalet serves as dining room, spa, and social space. Dinner is locally sourced Valaisian cooking — raclette, charcuterie, mountain cheese, Valais wine — served in a room with the wood-fire warmth and low light that Alpine evenings require. The spa is small but competent: sauna, hot tub, massage treatments available.

Summer is a different proposition but equally worth considering. Whitepod describes a summer getaway in the heart of the alpine pastures, and the hiking from Les Cerniers into the surrounding Alps covers terrain that the ski season buries. Wildflower meadows, ibex on the higher ridges, views across the Rhône Valley to the peaks beyond. The same environmental philosophy that governs the winter operation — minimal energy use, local sourcing, low-impact construction — applies to the summer experience, and the mountains at this altitude in July reward the visit regardless of the season that brought you here.
