Safari Desert Camp is a permanent Bedouin-style camp that opened in September 2008. It sits in the Wahiba Sands in the Bidiyah area of Oman, in a quiet, remote stretch of desert between two big dunes. About forty chalets are spread widely across the sand.

## The rooms

Two kinds of room are offered. The double or twin chalets are decorated in a traditional areesh style, with either a double bed or two single beds. The double or twin tents aim for the feel of a Bedouin camp and have the same choice of one double or two single beds. In both, the bathroom with toilet and shower is private. Carpets, pillows and a dressing table complete the furnishings.

The camp does not run on generators. Like the Bedouin, it uses only solar power, and it describes the whole camp as gentle on its natural surroundings.

## Arrival, food and evenings

The only way to reach the camp is a transfer by 4WD, and the camp uses its own vehicles and camels. The camp office is in Al-Mintirib, opposite the Shell fuel station. On arrival, guests are welcomed at a shaded reception of bamboo tables and cushioned chairs, where the manager serves Omani coffee (kahwa), mint tea, dates and cooled water and explains the activities and facilities.

The restaurant is laid out as an Omani majlis, with pillows and low tables. Dinner features a range of salads, an Omani barbecue with vegetables and rice or Omani bread, fresh fruit, dates and the traditional dessert, Omani halwa. Tea, coffee, cooled water and soft drinks are available in the majlis. After sunset, guests can retire to the majlis, built in Omani style with carpets, pillows, handrests and low tables.

## Things to do

Activities include camel trekking, traditional dancers, oud music and a buffet lunch. Tours run from a half-day trip through one-, two- and three-day itineraries.

The camp is owned and run by Ali Salem, who guides guests through the sands and is keen for them to understand Omani tradition and culture.

If you are planning a visit, ask the camp which kind of room, chalet or tent, suits you better.
