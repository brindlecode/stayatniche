Kirkenes Snowhotel is part of Snow Resort Kirkenes, in the far north of Norway near the Russian border. Its rooms are carved from ice and snow by ice artists, and the resort says it is open for overnight stays all year round, so the winter-wonderland effect is available in summer too. The snow hotel was founded in 2006 by Kåre Tannvik, a local man inspired by the snow caves he built as a child.

## Sleeping in snow

Only some of the accommodation is made of snow. Guests who choose the Snowhotel sleep in thermal sleeping bags in rooms shaped by hand. A heated lounge, with a sauna and showers, is nearby for the warm part of the evening. The resort also offers Gamme cabins, wooden huts inspired by the traditional Sámi hunting and fishing huts of Lapland, for those who prefer a warm bed.

## Food and animals

A restaurant serves Nordic dinners, and breakfast and a three-course dinner are included in the stay. Huskies and reindeer live on site, and husky sledding is among the activities, along with king crab safaris and northern lights trips offered in packages.

## Getting there

The resort lies at Sandnesdalen 14, in Bjørnevatn, outside Kirkenes. Direct flights from Oslo take about two hours.

## Good to know

Packages on the resort's site cover autumn aurora trips, summer midnight-sun stays and longer Arctic adventures. If you want to try the ice room itself, check which accommodation your package includes. The resort's site has a transfers section for arranging the journey.
