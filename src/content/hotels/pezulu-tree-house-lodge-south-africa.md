Pezulu Tree House Lodge is a small game lodge in the Greater Kruger area around Hoedspruit, in South Africa's Limpopo Province. Its accommodation is made up of treehouses, each one built around the canopy of growing trees. The lodge is kept intentionally small so that each guest has a personal experience.

## The main building

The thatched main building is interwoven with a large Marula tree. Inside are a lounge, a reception, a dining area and a bush bar.

## Evenings and downtime

A rock pool with loungers offers relief on hot summer days. Once the heat of the sun fades, guests can take a drink at the panoramic lookout point and watch the sun set over the Drakensberg mountains. Dinner is a three-course meal served under the stars around a log fire in the boma. Guests marking a special occasion are invited to ask the lodge about it.

## The setting

Pezulu sits in game-rich country close to Kruger National Park and the Panorama Route. The lodge describes a wide variety of experiences for both those looking for excitement and those looking for quiet.

## Practical notes

The lodge is on Guernsey Road in Hoedspruit. When you book, ask about the activities on offer and which treehouse suits your group.
