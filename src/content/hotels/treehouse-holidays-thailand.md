Treehouse Holidays is a small, owner-built place on Koh Yao Noi, the island in the Andaman Sea between Phuket and Krabi. It has eight houses, each of a different design and in a different price range, set by a 70-metre lake at the edge of the forest. They are separate houses raised well off the ground: the lowest described sits about 3.5 metres up and the highest about 6 metres.

## The houses

The two largest are the same size, 4 by 7 metres, with a second floor inside: a double bed in the main room and a single bed upstairs. Each has a kitchen with sink, fridge and cooking ware for three, a handcrafted shower and toilet room, a safe, a fan, and a veranda with a table and chairs. House no. 1 stands right at the lakeside and is the highest of all, about 6 metres above the ground, with a view over the lake and out to sea. House no. 2 sits a little further in, between the trees, about 4 metres above ground level.

The Family Bamboo Treehouse has two levels, with one bed downstairs and two upstairs, and a veranda more than 3 metres long. The Little Bamboo Treehouse sits about 3.5 metres off the ground and is made for two, with bamboo beds and a view through the roof. The Open Treehouse is a smaller house for one or two with an open construction that lets the breeze through. In these three the toilet and shower are outside, in front of the house.

## Around the lake

There is a roofed yoga space for up to 20 people, a massage cottage, and a breakfast and barbecue house by the lake. The owners keep an organic garden, and its bananas, papayas and other fruit are free for guests. The nearest beaches are a few hundred metres away, and a village with small supermarkets, banks, a post office and restaurants is 500 metres off.

## Getting there

From Phuket Airport it is about 15 minutes by taxi to Bang Rong Pier, then a boat to Koh Yao Noi, where pick-up taxis wait at Manoh pier. From Krabi, boats leave from Krabi pier. On the island, ask for the treehouses at Laem Sai / Laem Kor.

## Practical notes

The houses are not recommended for children under 10. Ask the owner which house suits you: they differ a good deal in size, height and how open they are.
