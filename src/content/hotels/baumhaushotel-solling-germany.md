Baumhaushotel Solling is a treehouse hotel in the forest of the Solling, in the Weserbergland of Lower Saxony. It opened in 2008 and was the first treehouse hotel in the state. Its guests come from near and far.

## The setting

The hotel lies in the Solling-Vogler nature park, in the heart of the Weserbergland. The idea is to get out into nature and away from mass tourism, whether as a couple, with family or with friends. The company is based in Uslar, and the Hesse and North Rhine-Westphalia borders are not far away.

## The treehouses

There are ten treehouses. All of them are heated, they sleep between two and five people, and they can be booked all year round. Each has its own name: Baumtraum, Freiraum, Refugium, Kobel, Rundhaus, Burg, Sternengucker, Sollingblick, Ahletal and Aurora. Breakfast can be added to a booking, up to three days before arrival.

## Tree tents

For guests who want more adventure, there are tree tents. Each one is stretched between three trees, beeches or spruces, and hangs about 1.5 metres above the forest floor. They are a way to experience the woods from the inside.

## Romance in winter

The hotel's treehouses have long been a retreat for couples, and the hotel says that anniversaries, wedding nights and a number of proposals have taken place there. In the winter season the Kobel treehouse is offered as a romance package for two nights. It includes a chilled bottle of sparkling wine on arrival (alcohol-free on request), a music box for your own playlist, a dozen LED candles, a duvet folded into a heart shape and a corkscrew as a keepsake. After booking, guests receive a personalised 3D fold-out card as a voucher.

## Practical notes

The hotel lends two handcarts to guests for free, so bags, drinks and books are easy to carry to the treehouses or tree tents. Online booking is available through the hotel's website, and gift vouchers for a night, two nights or a tree tent can be bought there too.
